1. Information we collect

  • How you sign in. You can sign in with Apple, with your Sleeper username, with ESPN, or with MyFantasyLeague (MFL). Each method creates a Fleeced account with an internal account ID. Depending on the method, we store:
    • Apple: the stable identifier Apple issues for our app, the name you choose to share (used as your display name), and a one-way (SHA-256) hash of the email address Apple shares with us. We don't keep the email address itself, and we never receive your Apple password.
    • Sleeper: your Sleeper user ID, username, display name and avatar, looked up from Sleeper's public API. A username alone shows public league information. Private boards and account features need a verified Sleeper session (see "Platform connections" below).
    • ESPN: your ESPN member ID (called SWID), which ESPN includes in league data.
    • MFL: your MFL username.
  • League data. From the platforms you connect we read your leagues, rosters, draft picks and league settings. We also read your leaguemates' usernames, display names and rosters, and your league's trades and roster history. We use leaguemates' data only to run trade discovery and league features for members of that league.
  • Your rankings and in-app activity. We store:
    • every ranking comparison you make (three-player matchups, tier saves, manual reorders, pick-anchor answers);
    • the per-player ratings computed from them, and rating history over time;
    • rankings you import from a file you upload or paste;
    • trade likes and passes, trade cards shown to you, mutual trade matches and "bad trade" flags, including any reason or optional note you give when you pass on a trade or flag one;
    • your answers in Calibration, where you grade suggested trades;
    • players you skip or mark untouchable or target;
    • team-outlook and position preferences, Win Now scenarios, and team-overhaul plans you create.

    We also record activity events (sign-in, ranking, trade actions) with a device snapshot: device type, OS version and app version.

  • Usage analytics (first-party only). To understand where the app is confusing or broken, the app and website send us usage events: screens viewed, sign-in and setup steps completed, taps on core features, how long actions take, and app errors. Error messages are scrubbed of personal data and shortened before they leave your device. These events are processed and stored only on our own server; no analytics company receives them.
    • Device identifier. Each install generates a random device identifier (a meaningless ID like dev_a1b2…) so we can count installs and see where new users get stuck before they sign in. It contains no information about you or your device. In the iOS app it is kept in the device's secure storage; on the website, in your browser's local storage; in the browser extension, in the extension's own storage.
    • Session identifier. Events also carry a session identifier, which we store only as a one-way hash.
    • Experiments. If we run product experiments, the variant you saw is recorded with these events.
    • Country. Usage events from the app and website also record a two-letter country code (for example "US"), supplied in a network header by our hosting provider's network. It is never more precise than the country, and we never work out your location from your IP address ourselves.
  • Server monitoring. When the app calls our server, we keep a short record of the request, including your account ID, to find and fix errors. These records are deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Feedback and messages to us.
    • In-app feedback: we store the note text, the screen it was sent from, a severity label, and device metadata (app version, platform, device type, OS version). Feedback can be sent anonymously; if you're signed in, it's linked to your account. The app also keeps a local copy on your device.
    • Our website's contact form: we receive your message and topic, linked to your account if you're signed in on the website.
    • Email: we receive your email address, your message and any attachments. Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail; see section 4).

    We use these only to answer you, handle your request and improve the Service.

  • Push notification tokens. If you turn on push notifications, we store an opaque Expo push token and your platform so we can deliver alerts. We also store your notification preferences and your time zone, which we use for quiet hours and for daily streaks.
  • Platform connections.
    • Sleeper. To verify that a Sleeper account is yours, or to send trades, you sign in on Sleeper's own site, in the app or the browser extension. Your Sleeper password is entered on Sleeper's site; we never see, receive or store it.
      • The app captures the session token Sleeper issues and we store it encrypted at rest. The encryption key lives only in our server environment, never in the database, and the server never returns the token to the app or website.
      • The iOS app may also keep the token in the device's secure storage to re-verify you. The extension uses it only briefly and doesn't save it.
      • This token is a full-account Sleeper credential. We use it to verify ownership and, when you ask, to submit or withdraw trade proposals.
      • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Leagues, which deletes the stored token immediately.
    • ESPN. To connect ESPN, you log in on ESPN's own page inside the app; we never see your password. We keep two ESPN sign-in cookies:
      • espn_s2, stored encrypted;
      • SWID, which is your ESPN member ID and is stored as is.

      We use them to import your ESPN leagues and to send trades you approve. You can disconnect in Settings → Leagues, which deletes both cookies.

    • MFL. When you sign in with MFL, your password passes through our server to MFL's sign-in once, to start a session. We never store it. We keep the session MFL returns, encrypted, and use it to read your leagues and to send trades you approve. You can disconnect in Settings → Leagues.
  • Crash and performance data (Sentry). The iOS app uses Sentry to report crashes and performance traces (a sample of app interactions). Reports include device model, OS version and app version. After sign-in they're tagged with an account identifier (your Fleeced account ID, Sleeper user ID or ESPN member ID, depending on how you signed in) so we can look into your bug reports. They never carry your name or email address. The tag is cleared when you sign out. Sentry is set up not to collect other personal data by default. Sentry may also record the IP address a report came from.
  • Cookies and local storage.
    • Our website sets no cookies.
    • It uses your browser's local storage for your sign-in token, your account and selected league, the device identifier, queued usage events and, if you arrived through an invite link, the inviter's Sleeper username.
    • The app keeps similar data in its own storage on your device.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We don't store passwords. Sleeper and ESPN passwords stay on their own sign-in pages. MFL passwords are forwarded to MFL once and never stored.
  • We don't ask for card numbers or payment-account passwords.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data. The only location-related data is the country code described in section 1.
  • No advertising or third-party marketing analytics tools. There are no ad networks and no trackers such as Google Analytics, Meta, Firebase Analytics or Mixpanel in the app or website. The usage analytics above are collected and stored by us alone. The only third party that receives diagnostic data is Sentry, for crashes and performance.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the product:
    • build your rankings;
    • generate trade suggestions;
    • match you with leaguemates on mutually liked trades;
    • show value trends;
    • send trade proposals you approve;
    • send the notifications you turned on.
  • Rankings and trade discovery. Trade suggestions work by comparing your player values with your leaguemates' values to find trades both sides should like.
    • Your raw ranking list is never shown to other users, but it does influence which trade suggestions your leaguemates see.
    • When you "like" a trade, the other manager may see an interest indicator on the mirrored trade, and a mutual like creates a match visible to both of you.
    • Leaguemates can also see an aggregate "contrarian score" beside your username: one number for how far your rankings differ from the league consensus per position. They never see which players you rank high or low.
  • AI-assisted matchup selection. To pick informative ranking matchups, we may send Anthropic's Claude API player names and details (position, team, age, experience) together with your anonymous rating signals for those players. These requests contain no account identifiers: no username, user ID or league name.
  • Sharing features.
    • Share images and shared trade pages contain only what you chose to share. Anyone with the link can view a shared page, and shared pages stay available so links keep working (see section 5).
    • Invite links you share include your Sleeper username so we can show who invited a new user.
    • If the public-profile feature is turned on, your profile page (at /u/your-username) would show your display name, avatar, league count, your saved tier snapshot and your top over- and under-consensus takes. It never shows private league data or other users' rankings. This feature is currently off.
  • To answer you: your messages, feedback and data requests.
  • To keep the Service working and improve it: crash reports, feedback, activity events and server monitoring.

4. Third parties

Platforms you connect. We don't control these companies, and their own privacy policies apply to what they do.

  • Sleeper: we read your public account and league data from Sleeper's API. With your opt-in, we send your trade proposals to Sleeper. On our website, player and user images load from Sleeper's image servers, which receive your IP address.
  • ESPN: with your connection, we read your ESPN leagues and send trade proposals you approve.
  • MyFantasyLeague (MFL): with your connection, we read your MFL leagues and send trade proposals you approve. Your MFL password passes to MFL's sign-in as described above.
  • Apple: if you use Sign in with Apple, Apple confirms your identity to us. Apple also delivers push notifications on iOS.

Service providers that process data for us:

  • Render: hosts our server and database in the United States (Oregon). Your data is stored on Render's infrastructure, and Render keeps standard server request logs.
  • Expo, and Apple/Google push services (APNs / FCM): deliver push notifications. They process your push token and the notification content, which can include leaguemates' usernames.
  • Sentry: receives crash and performance reports from the iOS app, as described above.
  • Anthropic: receives player details and anonymous rating signals for matchup selection, as described above.
  • Google: hosts our support mailbox (Gmail), which stores the emails you send us. It also serves our website's fonts, which means Google receives your IP address; the app bundles its fonts and makes no such request.

Data sources. These receive no data about you:

  • DynastyProcess: publicly published consensus player values, used to seed ratings.
  • Other published consensus values: publicly available player values from other sources, blended into the starting ratings.
  • nflverse: public NFL schedule data, used for bye weeks.

Where data is processed. We and our service providers process data in the United States.

We are not affiliated with any of the companies named in this section.

5. Data retention

  • Account and league data (profile, leagues, rosters): kept while your account is active, so the Service works across sessions.
  • Rankings, rating history and daily consensus value snapshots: kept for as long as your account exists. Historical values power trends, history charts and season recaps, and once a day passes those numbers can't be rebuilt.
  • Ranking comparisons, trade decisions, trade cards shown to you, and activity and usage events: append-only logs kept for product function and quality analysis while your account exists. On account deletion they're deleted or anonymized as described in section 6.
  • Server monitoring records with your account ID: deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Feedback and contact-form messages: kept through triage and afterwards as a product record. On account deletion, your account link and note text are removed.
  • Emails to our support mailbox: kept as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of your request.
  • Push tokens: kept until your account is deleted. Turning notifications off in Settings or on your device stops alerts.
  • Platform connections (Sleeper, ESPN, MFL): kept encrypted until any of these happens:
    • you disconnect (they're deleted immediately);
    • your account is deleted;
    • we find that the platform has expired or rejected them.

    An expired connection we haven't used again stays stored until one of those happens.

  • Shared trade pages and share snapshots: kept so links keep working. If you delete your account, they stay available with your account link removed; email us to remove one.
  • Sign-in sessions: end after a period of inactivity. Saved sessions end after 90 days without use, at sign-out, or when your account is deleted.
  • Server logs used for debugging are short-lived. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs under its own policy.
  • Backups: our database host keeps point-in-time recovery backups for 7 days. Data you delete can remain in those backups until they roll off, for up to 7 days. We use backups only to recover the Service from a failure.

6. Deletion, export and your choices

  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Settings → Account & data → Delete account.
    • How it runs: deletion waits for any account operation in progress to finish. If that takes too long, you'll be asked to try again in a minute.
    • What it removes:
      • your account record and sign-in links, including Sign in with Apple;
      • rankings and rating history, ranking comparisons, trade decisions, trade cards shown to you, Calibration answers, Win Now scenarios and preferences;
      • notifications, notification settings and push tokens;
      • activity and usage events, and linked anonymous usage history;
      • saved sign-in sessions;
      • stored Sleeper, ESPN and MFL connections.
    • What is anonymized instead, so the other manager's history stays intact:
      • trade matches keep the other side's record, with your identifier replaced by a deleted-user placeholder;
      • references to you on other users' trade decks, and "bad trade" flags, are cleared;
      • feedback you sent keeps its triage status, with your account link and note text removed;
      • shared trade pages stay available, with your account link removed;
      • rank sets you shared publicly stay available, credited to "Deleted contributor";
      • referral records keep the referral, with your account link removed.
    • What may remain:
      • server monitoring records, until they expire within 30 days;
      • backup copies, until they roll off within 7 days;
      • messages you sent through our website's contact form without signing in, which can't be matched to your account automatically. Email us to have them deleted;
      • if you invited someone, their account may still list your Sleeper username as their inviter. Email us to have it removed;
      • team-overhaul plans you created, which aren't yet removed by in-app deletion. Email us and we'll delete them;
      • public league records on Sleeper, ESPN or MFL, and other managers' records, which may still mention you.
  • If you're signed in with an ESPN or MFL league that hasn't been confirmed as yours, Delete account signs you out, removes this device's sign-in link, and deletes the usage events recorded from this device. Your rankings, trade decisions, trade cards shown to you and league data from that sign-in stay with that league's account, because they may belong to the team's real owner. To ask about them, email fleecedapp@gmail.com; you'll be asked to confirm the account is yours.
  • If you can't delete in the app (for example, you can't sign in), see "Requests outside the app" below.
  • Export your data. In the app, Settings → Account & data lets you download a copy of the data we hold about you (profile, rankings, decisions, events) as a machine-readable file.
    • It includes linked account aliases and recommendation records.
    • Sign-in tokens, encrypted credentials, push tokens, raw billing records and short-lived server monitoring records are left out.
    • Team-overhaul plans are not yet included; email us for a copy.
  • Requests outside the app. For deletion, correction, or any privacy question when you can't use the app, email fleecedapp@gmail.com from the address you'd like us to reply to.
    • Tell us how you sign in (Apple, Sleeper, ESPN or MFL) and your username, or the email on your Apple ID, so we can find your records.
    • We check that a request comes from the account's owner before we act. For example, we may ask you to write from the email on your Apple ID, or to sign in to the app once so the request can be confirmed there. We don't act on requests we can't verify.
    • We never send account data by email. A copy of your data is available only through the in-app export, which needs you to be signed in.
    • We acknowledge requests within 2 business days and complete them within 30 days.
    • Our website's contact form has a "Delete my data" option. The form can't send replies, so we act on a form request only when it comes from a signed-in account we can verify. To hear back, email us.
  • Correct your data. Most account and league data comes from the platforms you connect: change it there, and we pick up the change. For anything else, email us.
  • Turn things off at any time:
    • push notifications: per category in Settings, or entirely in your device settings;
    • platform connections: Settings → Leagues;
    • sign out: ends your session on that device and clears the crash-report tag.
  • Device identifier after deletion. In the iOS app, the random install identifier lives in the device's secure storage, which apps can't fully erase. It stays on the device after account deletion and survives reinstalls. After deletion it links to nothing on our servers. If you sign up again from the same device, we see a new user on a device we've seen before, nothing more. On the website, clearing your browser's site data removes it.
  • Your rights.
    • Everyone can access, export, correct and delete their data as described above, and withdraw consent (for example, to push notifications) at any time.
    • Residents of some US states, such as California, may have additional rights under state law, for example to know what we collect and to opt out of the sale of personal information or targeted advertising. We do neither.
    • To use any of these rights, email us. We won't treat you differently for using them.
