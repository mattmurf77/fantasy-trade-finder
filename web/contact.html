Data requests

Quickest route: in the app, open Settings › Account & data and choose Download my data or Delete account.

If you can't use the app, email fleecedapp@gmail.com from the address you'd like the reply sent to, and say how you sign in and your username. You'll be asked to confirm the account is yours, and account data is never sent by email. Requests are acknowledged within 2 business days and completed within 30 days.

You can also pick Delete my data above while signed in on this site. The form can't send a confirmation.
