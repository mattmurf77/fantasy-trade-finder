How trades are priced

Your rankings set the prices. Here's how it works, and the ways to rank.

Every trade is priced on two boards — yours and your leaguemate's. A trade surfaces when each side gives up less value on their own board than they get back; the fairness meter compares the two packages by value.

Rank more players and trades get priced off your board instead of consensus.

Ways to rank

Pick whichever fits. You can switch any time from the Rank tab in the app.

  • Quick set

    Fastest Recommended

    Tap players into value tiers, one tier at a time. A usable board in a few minutes per position.

    Then, if you want: Quick rank — a follow-on pass that fine-tunes the order inside each tier. Offered when you finish.

  • Head-to-heads

    Most precise

    Put three players in order at a time — a few seconds each, and your board sharpens with every answer.

  • Tiers board

    Most control

    Your whole board as drag-and-drop value tiers — every placement is your call, from 4+ 1sts down to FA.

More ways to rank

  • Pick Anchors

  • Overall ranks

    Order every player 1-to-N yourself — drag rows or tap a rank number
