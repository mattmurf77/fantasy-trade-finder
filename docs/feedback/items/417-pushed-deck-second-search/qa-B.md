## Undisclosed costs — summary

1. **Discoverability of "search everything" on an anchored deck** dropped from a full-width primary to a receipt text link (B-4). Deliberate and correct per D-158/ice-rationing, but not stated in the PRD as a cost.
2. **On-screen copy now lies in two places** on the pushed fair deck (B-1) and the page **invites a tap on a disabled button for ~1 s** (B-5). The PRD's "no copy changed" line (§6 Chalkline) is true and is exactly the problem: the copy needed to change with the control it quotes.
3. **"One primary per page":** improved, not broken — the anchored page now has zero page-level primaries; the card's ✓/✕ carry the action. The deck-summary card keeps its own single primary (Done) per its PRD §5.3 button budget (`:8611-8618`). No design-system regression.

## What I checked and did not find

- No path clears `fairDeck` without also emptying the deck other than the fair sweep's own failure exit (`:3594`, deck already empty) and the two inline resets (`:1146`, `:2193`), which empty it themselves. The receipt cannot outlive its deck.
- `handleKeepSide` (`:3492`) and `handleSuppressionUndo` (`:3688`) already reset before dispatching; the prefs-changed strip (`:6619`) and unpin-retry (`:3659`) route through `handleFindTrades`. No un-reset model dispatch from a fair deck remains.
- The `resetDeckForNewTargets` inside `handleFindTrades` does not touch the pin store (assertion 8f), `sheetOpponent`, or `inlineAnchor` — so Clear keeps the partner and the anchor label is merely hidden, ready for the next fair deck on this instance (none can happen on the pushed page — it would be a new push — so no stale-label risk).
- Batching: the choke's `resetDeckForNewTargets()` (disarm) and `runFairPackages` (arm) run in the same effect tick; React 18 batches to a net `true`. The two disarms both sit after the epoch guard, so a superseded sweep never flips the flag — the intended #330 R-10 posture, and the reason B-2's bypassing bump matters.
- No new testID, event, flag, route, or schema; `testid-lint` unaffected; the change is byte-identical for flag-off, team/player modes and the landing (all three `findCtaHiddenForAnchoredDeck`/`fairSweepPending` reads are `false` there by construction).
