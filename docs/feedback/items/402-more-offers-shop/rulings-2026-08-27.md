## R-2026-08-27-B — O-2 (entry point): shop IS "more offers"; #402 and #403 are one experience

Operator: **"Shop players and more offers are effectively the same concept.
The shop players can be accessed from the 'more offers' button (depending on
whether there is one or more players on the give side). I like the swipeable
tiles presented per trade from the feedback on 402."**

Implications, stated plainly:

1. **The Matches long-press entry (`lld-delta.md` §4.4) is no longer the
   front door.** The primary entry is the **"more offers"** control — #402's
   surface. Whether the long-press row survives as a secondary entry is an
   open design question for the joint plan, not a settled spec. (If it does
   survive, the give-side guard ruling below still applies.)
2. **#403 is no longer a standalone wave behind #402 — the two items are one
   feature and must be planned together.** The brief's constraint ("#402's
   decisions win on any shared surface") escalates from a copy rule to a
   structural one.
3. **Give-side conditionality:** the operator ties behavior to how many
   players sit on the give side. Single give player → "more offers" can open
   shop for that player directly; multiple → needs a design answer (picker,
   or per-player affordance). To be settled in the joint #402/#403 plan.
4. **Presentation:** the swipeable per-trade tiles from the #402 feedback are
   the liked pattern; #403's pager/`1 / X` design should conform to it, not
   the other way around.
