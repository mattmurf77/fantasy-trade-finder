# Reusable evaluation artifact templates

Copy the relevant section into an initiative/model/release artifact. These are blank forms, not signed approvals. Keep private data behind manifest hashes and access-controlled locations.

## Ratification decision

- Product/evaluation/analytics signatories and date:
- Charter/rubric/evaluator version and incumbent baseline manifest:
- Dimension-specific anchors and adjudicated examples:
- Critical segments and rationale; acceptable Unknown coverage:
- Noninferiority/practical-effect margins and baseline evidence:
- Fixed eligibility, assignment unit, origin/episode/dedup/carry-in rules:
- Outcome/post-milestone/completion windows, ingestion lag and coverage:
- Attrition/confirmation limits, power/MDE/stopping method:
- Unratified items, restricted experiment permissions and expiry:

## Evidence/data card

- Source/population/dynasty filter, rights and privacy owner:
- Capture/source time, ownership/format, immutable hash and storage:
- Field coverage, purged/unreconstructable inputs, response selection:
- Dedup/overlap, lineage/time/manager/concept split and permitted use:
- Retention/deletion propagation and access roles:

## Model development brief

- Problem and falsifiable benefit to each manager:
- Model/arm/code/config/artifact and component changed:
- Features available as-of inference; personal/market/outlook roles:
- Baselines/ablations, compute envelope, common presentation policy:
- Predeclared metric IDs/margins and failure risks:
- Training/tuning/calibration/locked-test manifests and access policy:

- Rationale, exceptions, inputs and ordering/mutation behavior:

- Correctness tests, deliberate defects detected, critical slices:

## Online experiment specification

- Enrollment frozen before assignment; activity lookback/measurement support:
- Cluster unit/spillover, randomization/logging probabilities, SRM check:
- Primary fixed-cohort yield; no-request/error/empty handling:

- Exposure stages, intervention identity, rollout permissions:

## Production scorecard

- Version tuple, observation cutoff, cohort/sampling/maturity:
- Construction/policy/display funnel; fixed-cohort outcome and attrition:
- Six-dimension four-side plus two-package distributions:
- Numerator/denominator/Unknown/N/A/cluster uncertainty for each metric:
- Critical slices, changed input mix, anomalies, examples and owners:
- Limitations and decision; no weighted overall quality total:

## Error/incident and decision log

- Evidence ID, affected manager/direction/dimension and actual symptom:
- Root component, source/telemetry versus modeling/policy cause:
- Counterexample and new regression; observed impact/coverage:
- Containment/fix/rollback authorization and verification:
- Holdout impact, follow-up owner and deadline:

Charter/metric/component registry forms are the canonical files in this folder, not copies inside each model. All records must preserve negative findings and evidence limitations.
