# Evaluation charter — draft 1

**Objective:** discover packages both managers would willingly execute, selecting targets and sells through each manager's personal rankings plus outlook/needs, then constructing defensible market terms. Higher-impact assets and stud compensation support this objective; they do not replace it.

The unit is an exact package in frozen context, graded as A give, A receive, A package, B give, B receive, B package for each of six dimensions. The weakest manager remains visible. Do not reduce the six dimensions to one weighted total.

Selected outlook outranks inferred outlook. Explicit exploration is not blocked by the evaluator. Missing rankings fall back to consensus in generation but cannot count as demonstrated personal conviction. Trade feedback must not move an explicit tier. User tiers and pick equivalents remain the product vocabulary; numerical source values in private diagnostics are not new user-facing prices.

Separate three layers: constructor/internal search; runtime validation/filtering/order/injection; observed product outcomes. An independent evaluator may share identity/tier parsers but not use the model's own utility as its answer. Generated, committed, rendered, responded, mutually interested, confirmed and provider-completed are different facts.

Like/queue/send means expressed willingness toward exact displayed terms. It does not reveal every silent manager's willingness, and send is not completion. KTC/DP are reference prices; completed dynasty Sleeper trades are a positive-only exchange corpus. Neither supplies absent refusal/private-preference labels.

The framework begins with correctness invariants and an honest incumbent baseline. Product and independent evaluation owners then sign empirical margins, critical segments, evidence coverage and power assumptions before challenger tuning. Until ratification, reports are descriptive/evidence-limited. The already-live model is not retroactively certified.

Named human owners, permitted data retention and numerical promotion margins must be assigned in the ratification record. Agents building tooling are not substitutes for these ongoing accountabilities.
