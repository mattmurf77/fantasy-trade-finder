## Phase 1 — Current-state map (what the model IS today)

One file:line-cited document covering, at minimum:

1. **Generation paths and their flags:** v2 scoring (`backend/trade_service.py`) vs v3 package search
   (`backend/trade_optimizer.py`) vs `trade_gen_v2` (built DARK, `backend/trade_gen_v2.py`); which path
   serves which surface (deck, finder targeting, calculator, likes-you injection).
2. **The gate stack in order:** dual-board epsilon gain (#108 `user_gain_epsilon`), consolidation
   discount, consensus fairness band, presentment rules R1 (overpay ceiling), R2 (pos_net_cap), R3
   (pick-is-the-gap), R4 (awaiting/matched exclusion), R5 (need gate) — plus the D-159 knockout additions
   and their `model_config` knobs.
3. **Value inputs:** Elo→value mapping, pool calibration (`trade.pool_calibration`), divergence vs
   consensus basis selection and fallback (`trade.divergence_fallback`), pick pricing (flat round-1
   D-079, year decay flags, round-2 recalibration, slot labels display-only per Q-023).
4. **Learning layers:** Thompson deck (`trade.thompson_deck`), acceptance-prior multiplier, exposure
   caps, negmem (dark, ADR-015), pass-cooldown, fatigue — what actually updates from user behavior today
   vs what is static.
5. **What the model does NOT see:** league scoring settings (SF/TEP premium handling?), roster-context
   beyond need-fit, liquidity/age preferences — honest absences list.

## Phase 3 — Gripe-to-hypothesis matrix (test OUR model against the community's complaints about the incumbents)

| # | Community gripe (evidence in §11 doc) | Hypothesis about OUR model | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | "KTC waaaay overvalues picks"; rookie fever; far-year firsts priced like current | Our pick pricing (flat round-1 across years, D-079) may over-offer far-year firsts → user passes | Pass-rate on cards with far-year firsts vs matched player-only cards; review D-079 against outcomes |
| H2 | Consolidation trap ("three WR3s = one Jefferson") | Consolidation discount + R1 + filler floors prevent junk-stuffing | Accept/like on several-for-one vs 1:1; kill-rate bands from the G6 DB-1/DB-2 work as baseline |
| H3 | Youth hype / veteran undervaluation | Presented cards may skew young on the receive side | Age distribution of offered vs received players in liked vs passed cards |
| H4 | Knee-jerk volatility (values overreact to news) | Our consensus refresh cadence + Elo dynamics may lag or overreact | Value-update cadence audit; deck churn after news events; freshness = the moat (§11), so measure staleness |
| H5 | Format blindness (SF QB premium, TEP) | Do we price QBs/TEs by league format at all? | Code-verify format inputs; compare QB card like-rates in SF vs 1QB leagues |
| H6 | Liquidity/context blindness (window, roster construction) | need-fit + lanes + outlook cover this — partially lit | Like-rate for need-aligned vs non-aligned cards; window-consistent vs not |
| H7 | Personalization demand ("flat values don't work") | Divergence-basis cards outperform consensus-basis | Like/accept by basis, controlling for board coverage — if this is not clearly positive, the core pitch needs work before scale |
| H8 | "KTC-fair" weaponization / insult offers | Insult-rate guardrail holds under all candidates | Re-run insult rule on every candidate's decks (Phase 4) |

## Phase 4 — Champion selection

**Candidates:** (a) incumbent prod config (v2/v3 + G6 + D-159 knockouts as lit today); (b) incumbent +
remaining knockout programme items (R5 dual-need rescue → consolidation bundle → R2 starter-depth, per
NEXT.md order); (c) `trade_gen_v2` (dark); (d) any of the above + negmem (respecting PRD §8.2 round-boundary
rule GR3 and the unrun TestFlight checklist).

**Method, in order of evidence strength available now:**
1. **Offline replay on recorded impressions** (the F8 offline-eval PRD in `docs/plans/tiktok-discovery/`
   is the spec; the deck-outcome-validation and knockout-refine harnesses exist — reuse, don't rebuild).
2. **Deck-eval gate** on every candidate: empty-deck <5%, insult <3% (same rules as 2026-08-15 report),
   plus like-rate and H1–H8 deltas.
3. **Live read only after offline winnow:** interleaving-style comparison if feasible at our population
   (~100x sample-efficient vs A/B); otherwise sequenced default with before/after readout. No A/B theater
   at 3–5 actives.

**Output:** a champion recommendation with (i) the measured case, (ii) flag/knob flip list with deploy-free
kill switches, (iii) what stays dark and why, (iv) TEST_LEDGER entries drafted. Operator makes the flips —
this thread recommends, it does not light flags.
