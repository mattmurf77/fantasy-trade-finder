## 4. Analysis (vs FTF Pro $6.99/mo and $34.99/yr, Founder $119 lifetime, Season Pass $24.99, tips $1.99–$9.99)

1. **The $30–50/yr band is not empty, but nothing dynasty-specific sits below $49.99.** Dynasty Scout and DynastyDealer are both $49.99/yr on iOS. FantasyPros PRO is $47.88/yr but excludes dynasty. FTF at $34.99 would be the cheapest annual dynasty subscription found; the next cheapest dynasty-capable plans are $49.99 (iOS apps) and $71.88–$96 (FantasyPros MVP, Dynasty Nerds, Footballguys, Draft Sharks).
2. **Monthly**: $6.99 matches Dynasty Scout ($6.99), sits under Dynasty Nerds ($7.99), and is above DynastyDealer ($5.99). The monthly band is crowded at $6–8.
3. **Lifetime**: no competitor shows a lifetime plan. $119 is about 1.5× a year of Dynasty Nerds ($79.99) and 1.3× Footballguys Elite ($89.99).
4. **Season Pass $24.99** sits between Dynasty Nerds Rookie Guide ($19.99), PlayerProfiler Rookie Guide ($25) and Dynasty Trade Lab's 75-credit Season pack ($29.99), and below UDK ($34.99).
5. **Trials**: FTF's 14-day and 1-month trials are much longer than any found. FantasyPros and Dynasty Scout offer 3 days, Dynasty Nerds none, and RotoWire an unspecified "Free Trial".
6. **Trade tools or league sync paywalled**:
   - Dynasty Nerds: 3 trades/day and 1 league free; full access $7.99/mo or $79.99/yr.
   - FantasyPros: trade analyzer in all tiers from $47.88/yr; trade suggestions and dynasty need MVP (implied $71.88/yr); sync capped at 2, 10 or 50 leagues.
   - Footballguys: dynasty trade tools are ELITE, $89.99/yr.
   - RotoWire: My Leagues trade analyzers from $14.99/mo.
   - PFF: league sync from $9.99/mo.
   - Dynasty Trade Lab: about $0.40–$0.50 per credit.
   - Draft Sharks: dynasty calculator, tier not confirmed (likely $96/yr).
7. **Free anchors keep pulling the floor down.** KTC, FantasyCalc and Sleeper are free, and KTC now gives trade-sourced values away. DLF is effectively free through sportsbook promos. A paywalled trade calculator alone will not sell; personalized mutual-gain suggestions have to carry the price.
