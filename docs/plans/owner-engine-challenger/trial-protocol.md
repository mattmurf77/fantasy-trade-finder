# Owner construction trial: what will count as evidence

**Status:** validation protocol, not evidence of deployment, exposure or improvement.

## Hypothesis

Building simple exchanges around personal targets and complementary team
objectives will produce more actionable offers than optimizing inherited
multi-asset candidate sets. Market value governs terms; willingness does not
automatically increase the asking price.

## Controls and treatment

- `current`, `challenger` and `gen_v2` retain their identities and generator
  implementations. `owner_v1` is the new construction arm. Do not pool its
  outcomes with the older `challenger` merely because both are challengers.
- Organic jobs compare generated arms within the existing interleaved serving
  framework. Report arm/group position and available supply; group quotas do
  not imply equal exposure counts per arm. Distinguish candidate generation,
  policy rejection, drafting, serving, viewing and deciding.
- Selected searches need their own treatment/control attribution; they are
  not organic model-deck observations. Deterministic assignment must be
  server-owned, versioned and recorded, preserving the normalized request.
  Repeating the same context is not an independent randomized sample.
- The new model is not allowed to inherit legacy positive-personal-surplus
  requirements after it explicitly accepts an outlook/need-funded trade-off.
  That would erase the experiment before a user could see it. Actual legality,
  market bounds and exact-final-package verification still apply.

## Before exposure

1. Prove candidate-set changes with synthetic deterministic input: change only
   a deliberate ranking or outlook and compare canonical package identities,
   not display indices. Verify the same package retains its market pricing.
2. Verify the three existing controls' output/profile tests without recapturing
   any golden. New registry/config entries are not changed control behavior.
3. Trace selected SEND, selected GET, partner, More Offers and league buy/sell
   through the shared constructor. Test explicit selections, same-tier upgrades,
   missing rankings and owner-defined untouchable semantics separately.
4. Show that every exposed treatment has its frozen market/personal/input
   context and a valid impression ID. Verify private partner values are absent
   from public responses. Verify real views, passes and queue actions join it.
   Confirm `deck.signal_v2`, `analytics.ingest` and client-event ingestion are
   enabled; impression persistence without client views cannot supply the
   viewed denominator. Organic trial publication waits for real IDs.
5. Run bounded-pool/league runtime fixtures; report enumeration limits and
   truncated/empty opponents. A successful HTTP response with zero offers is
   not a successful quality test.
6. Complete parent and independent review, full automated gates, exact-head
   hosted CI and release verification. Mobile physical checks remain separately
   labelled until actually performed on the identified TestFlight build.
7. Verify the intended tester has installed 1.17.2 or later before enabling
   serving. EAS submission completion does not establish installation. Include
   may run in shadow first. There is no version allowlist on global serving:
   older clients can omit partial notices and selected exposure metadata or
   apply legacy sorting, so their results are not a clean test of this contract.

## Readout

Use one row per unique viewed impression and retain the original serve, view
and action timestamps. A manager seeing a trade days later has a different
exposure, not a retroactive view of the first manager's card.

For grouped selected responses, the stored impression `card_index` is the
flattened response occurrence, not necessarily the position in the active
More Offers tab. Use the viewed event's recorded index/screen for actual
fronted position; do not pool it with organic draft positions. Compatible
Same value/upgrade occurrences can have distinct impression IDs for one package.

Primary descriptive comparison: interested/queued/proposed impressions divided
by viewed impressions, with numerator and denominator. Report passes and pass
reason mix alongside it. Report matching and provider-confirmed execution as
separate downstream outcomes; no assumption that liking guarantees acceptance.

Stratify by surface, generator version, manager/league, package shape and deck
position. Show account fairness, personal-data availability and team outlook
where N permits. Identify duplicate concepts across arms and repeated offers;
they are not independent successful recommendations.

Safety/supply measures: percentage of simple packages, at least-three-player
sides, market-loss distribution, both-team objective evidence, no-result rate,
partial coverage, repeated headliners, generation time and budget exhaustion.
Keep generated-but-unviewed cards out of response-rate denominators. Keep
prefetched inactive More Offers pages out of view counts. Report missing view
instrumentation by client version rather than pretending it is a pass.

Small samples can justify fixing a concrete failure, but cannot establish a
winning model. Do not select a winner from raw pooled likes, compare unrelated
leagues as a causal before/after test, or count a successfully enabled flag as
proof that offers improved.

## Stop and rollback

Stop treatment exposure for privacy/ownership failures, a market-bound escape,
stale evidence after asset changes, silent selection loss or material latency
regression. Set `bakeoff_serve_owner=0`; optionally set include=0 to remove its
computation. Keep existing controls and all historical evidence. Quality
thresholds and numerical utility weights remain experimental until measured.
