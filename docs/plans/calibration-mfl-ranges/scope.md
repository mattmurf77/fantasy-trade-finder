# MFL Calibration native ranges and account-owned authentication

Date: 2026-11-07. Authorized follow-up to resolve feasible Calibration preparation failures. Separate from the fixed-slot repair and normal trade-model activation.

## Exact supported contract

The native `starters.count`, `iop_starters`, `idp_starters`, and each `position.limit` are captured together. Offensive and defensive totals must be explicit and sum to the total. Separate totals are observed provider fields, never inferred from a favored formation. Each permitted count vector is enumerated subject to all native minima, maxima and its partition total. Work is bounded to 32 slots, 100,000 candidate vectors and 256 legal templates per family. Unknown, contradictory, excessively large or coupled cross-domain rules remain unavailable.

The motivating aggregate observation has 20 starters: nine offense and eleven IDP; QB1–2/RB2–3/WR2–4/TE2–3 and DT1–3/DE2–3/LB2–4/CB2–3/S2–3. These produce seven offensive and twelve defensive templates. The observed aggregate export is the evidence for this shape; no private identifiers or customer rosters are copied into fixtures. A single pair of SUPER_FLEX slots would admit illegal third QBs/RBs/TEs and is not equivalent.

Both models maximize unique usable occupancy, then their existing dynasty lineup proxy, over the whole legal offensive family before and after each exchange. Their valuation policies remain distinct. The final common roster gate uses the same family and every positive subset demand `max(sum(min in S), total - sum(max outside S))`, preserving deficit and one-absence depth protections. It does not require every optional formation or invent backup requirements for zero-demand groups. Utility eligibility, QB diagnostics and the unchanged quality filter's QB/TE caps use attainable maxima across the family, not loose declared upper bounds. Default contexts keep their existing assignment, valuation and gate paths.

DT/DE/LB/CB/S retain distinct native positions and private native IDs, have no price or offense utility, remain untradeable and uncuttable, and retain active capacity cost with reserve/taxi exclusion. Known defensive assignment is diagnostic; absent availability never claims a filled slot. The unchanged fixed holdings prove no offensive trade changes defensive constraints. Salary/contracts, positional roster caps and other previously unsupported rules remain unsupported.

## Caller and canonical seat

A non-importing MFL account must supply its own stored credential. Fresh authenticated `myLeagues` must identify exactly one franchise in the requested league/season. No credential lookup for the importer, session mint, global binding rewrite, or public-read fallback is permitted. The existing canonical franchise map remains importer franchise→importer ID, every other franchise→synthetic MFL member ID. Rosters, owned picks and original-pick ownership use that map; personal rankings/preferences remain caller-account scoped. An explicitly stale arbitrary seat remains denied; an earlier caller-ID placeholder can resolve to the newly verified canonical seat.

Captured provenance records caller, native franchise, canonical map and credential revision without credentials. Before/after binding checks, source hashes and existing registered-participant deletion/incarnation fences remain required. No schema migration or implicit account linking is added. Existing exposed/answered cohorts remain immutable; only unexposed stale snapshots are eligible for reprepare under the established policy.
