This is not evidence that the remaining product-quality loss is harmless or that the candidate is qualified for release.

Any further semantic proposal requires its own parent specification and new independent evaluation, not fitting these retained outputs.

## 40: retained targets displaced by additional packages

Incumbent emits 152 offers; candidate emits 189. Neither filtering nor presentation changes either ordered inventory in this selected search. Eight incumbent first30 offers remain in candidate native inventory but move to ranks 31–50; eight candidate-only exact packages replace them. Thus the final prefix shares 22/30 offers with the incumbent prefix and native inventory. The departing offers contribute 6.5 favorable per-offer fractions; the eight entrants contribute zero. With 29 covered offers unchanged, this exactly accounts for the 22.41-point loss. Incoming known market-mass share actually increases 82.70%→83.95%; this is not simply fewer known offers.

Privacy-safe exact example: candidate rank 8 sends selected WR **40-S** (market 3605.64) for QB **40-Q** (3730.86) plus TE **40-T** (207.76). The same partner's WR-for-QB simple offer remains rank 1 in both arms. A personally favors the selected outgoing WR and disfavors the incoming QB; the TE is personally Unknown and not independently focal. Both B directions are Unknown. Adding the TE increases B's outgoing market consideration 5.57%, while A's outgoing terms are unchanged. This is an additional package, not an observed enthusiasm-induced increase in A's outgoing cost or a simpler-offer precedence inversion. Explicit selection is excluded from the generic term-precedence/presentation path; the earlier simple offer is an observation, not a claimed guard guarantee.

For contrast, displaced incumbent rank 23 remains candidate rank 32: the same selected WR buys a favored WR headliner (3990.83) plus an Unknown TE (216.57). This is a different target/partner comparison, not verified bilateral dominance. The selected outgoing authority also explains why A's 0% sell metric is not itself evidence that the tool disobeyed an outgoing preference. [The earlier development audit](development-audit.md) retained additional historical exact-offer analysis for four selected packages; it does not establish a reason for every newly observed absence.

## 63: favored does not necessarily mean significant

Native counts are 1325 incumbent versus 2183 candidate; significance survivors are 813 versus 1703. The common filter removes 13 of candidate native first30, including 12 offers with known favorable incoming focal assets. Exact native rank 1 in both arms sends a disfavored TE (306.82; personal/market tier 6) for a favored QB (261.06; personal tier 5, market tier 6). Both sides are below the second-round threshold, tier 4, despite perfect known A sell/buy direction. It is removed in both arms. B preferences are Unknown.

This establishes a filtering/prefix-replacement mechanism, not that significance should be weakened. Different native inventories and orders expose different replacement offers. Candidate policy3 first30 shares 29/30 packages with incumbent native and 18/30 with incumbent final first30; only one incumbent final-prefix package is absent from candidate native. Without rejection records, that absence cannot distinguish rejection from non-enumeration.

Policy3 then exchanges eight prefix occurrences without changing the 1703 survivors. Its smaller residual buy decline, 70.37%→68.52%, has 27 covered offers in both stages: favorable/known focal asset counts rise **19/27→20/30**, while incoming known market-mass share rises 87.98%→90.48%. More favorable assets can coexist with a lower mean of per-offer fractions when additional focal companions change the denominator. A sell alignment also declines 78.26%→73.91% at unchanged 23-offer coverage. B remains entirely Unknown; none of these movements establishes better or worse bilateral willingness.

## 67: a meaningful non-headliner changes the broader metric

Candidate significance and policy3 retain the same 1658 offers. Eight offers enter and eight leave first30. Seven entrants have perfect known A-buy focal fractions; the eighth has 1/2 versus the departing offers' 1/1. This additional half-hit offer accounts exactly for the 1.67-point residual decline. Total favorable/known focal counts nevertheless rise **33/34→35/37**; all 30 offers remain covered with 100% incoming known market-mass share.

That exact offer moves candidate significance rank 76→policy3 rank 20. It already exists at incumbent native rank 139/significance rank 96, so it is not new eligibility. A sends pick **67-P** (2377.41; personal preference Unknown) for favored maximum-market QB **67-Q** (1969.93; personal tier 2 versus market tier 3) plus nonmaximum TE **67-T** (692.12; personal/market tier 4, adverse within-tier order). Both incoming assets independently qualify as focal. B's personal directions are Unknown.

The same-partner pick-for-QB-only offer survives too, at candidate significance 918→policy3 909. Removing the TE changes incoming market consideration from 2662.05 to 1969.93 against the unchanged 2377.41 pick. Therefore the simple offer's perfect A-buy focal fraction is not proof of equivalent bilateral terms or a violated efficient-price edge. The promoted package retains a favored maximum-market headliner; its adverse nonmaximum companion lies outside policy3's maximum-headliner contract. The independent tier/order metric also differs from that contract's raw-value coordinate. The observed broader-metric decline therefore does not by itself demonstrate a broken policy3 comparison.
