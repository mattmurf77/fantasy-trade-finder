# Starter-impact alternatives: rankings and point projections

Research checked **2026-09-22** against primary provider/project documentation. Scope: lane 2 of [the parent research specification](spec-starter-impact-research.md). No account, purchase, bulk scrape, authenticated data request, package installation, or runtime/test change was made. Provider descriptions below are documented capabilities, not verified live payload coverage or measured accuracy. Sleeper verification and the internal integration design belong to the other two lanes.

## Decision-oriented shortlist

Use a **licensed raw-stat projection feed**, not dynasty prices or ordinal ranks, for quantitative starter-impact estimates. The conditional shortlist is:

| Candidate | Useful role | Decisive caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SportsDataIO | Weekly projections, including IDP; documented update workflow | Its legacy season-long feed is explicitly preseason-only, **not ROS**. Historical access is not an as-of revision archive. |
| RotoWire | Explicit weekly, full-season and remaining-season feeds | Written commercial/automated-use permission and payload validation required; current public docs do not settle future-week completeness or timestamp semantics. |
| FantasyPros official API | Consensus ranks plus advertised weekly/preseason/ROS stat projections | Separate commercial approval; direct-competition restriction needs explicit resolution for Fleeced. |
| nflverse / ffverse / DynastyProcess | ID crosswalks, rank archives, research/outcome infrastructure | Not an independently licensed, complete forward-projection feed; upstream rights and horizon semantics still apply. |

This is a procurement/validation shortlist, not an accuracy ranking. A normal consumer subscription or a publicly accessible page is not evidence that Fleeced may redistribute data, retain it indefinitely, or use it in a commercial trade model. Existing owner approvals for other sources do not cover these vendors.

## 1. FantasyPros: strong rank coverage, permission-gated projection candidate

The current official API product page advertises expert consensus rankings; NFL weekly, preseason and ROS projections; canonical player IDs/cross-references; news/injuries; and a commercial tier with historical/bulk access. It distinguishes free prototype access from personal noncommercial production access and commercial agreements. The advertised base is `/public/v2/json`; authentication uses an API-key header. [Official API offering](https://www.fantasypros.com/api-data/).

The older primary reference documents NFL projection fields such as passing/rushing/receiving counts and touchdowns, receptions, fumbles, and IDP tackles/assists/sacks/interceptions. It documents STD/PPR/HALF, offensive positions plus DST/K/IDP/DL/LB/DB, `week=0` preseason, and `fpid`/nullable `mflid`. **Do not implement that older `/v2/json` contract as if it were the current public API**: the current public documentation page did not expose readable schema in this browser, and no authenticated response was tested. Specifically, the old NFL parameter table does not establish the new ROS selector, exact included weeks, current IDP completeness, or per-row as-of fields. [Older official reference](https://api.fantasypros.com/v2/docs), [current documentation entry](https://api.fantasypros.com/public/v2/docs).

For ranks, the ROS page warns that its update marker is when revised submissions were checked; individual experts' publication dates determine their actual freshness. Retain horizon, scoring, contributing experts and their timestamps; a recent fetch is not proof of fresh consensus. Superflex/redraft ranking variants are separately identified in the maintained rank dictionary, but an SF rank remains ordinal—not a quarterback point multiplier. [FantasyPros ROS freshness note](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/ros-overall.php), [rank-field dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_ff_rankings.html).

**Access gate:** FantasyPros' support policy, updated August 5, 2026, requires a separate agreement for business use, redistribution and revenue-generating products; it also prohibits directly competing products and requires attribution. Player images are separately licensed and excluded. Obtain written approval specifically naming Fleeced's recommendation, derived-output, evaluation and retention uses before integration; HOF membership is insufficient. The linked default API terms are personal/noncommercial, so do not infer an exemption from the existence of a commercial sales tier. [Current access policy](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/49749297704475-How-do-I-request-access-to-the-FantasyPros-API), [default API terms](https://api.fantasypros.com/public/v2/terms-of-use).

Open questions: guaranteed weekly publication/correction cadence, full-roster rather than top-N coverage, fantasy-playoff cutoff, whether ROS includes the ongoing week, conditional-versus-unconditional injury treatment, source dependencies, and timestamped historical revisions. Commercial historical availability alone answers none of those.

## 2. SportsDataIO: clearest weekly/IDP operational contract

The NFL workflow says weekly player projections are game-specific by Tuesday 2 p.m. EST and updated every 15 minutes until kickoff. IDP game projections arrive Tuesday and change with news. Season-long and IDP season projections arrive around June, but the legacy full-season forecast stops being maintained once the regular season begins: it is **not updated ROS**. The guide separately distinguishes active roster status from gameday injury/availability. These are published workflow claims, not an SLA verified here. [NFL workflow](https://sportsdata.io/developers/workflow-guide/nfl).

The projection dictionary exposes a weekly game-stat structure, career-stable provider `PlayerID`, season/type/game identifiers and raw projected categories such as receptions and passing touchdowns; its projection table is labelled from 2013. A documented field/year is not proof every bench player or IDP stat is populated in every season. Validate required categories and use raw stats to apply league scoring rather than inheriting a DFS site's point column. The dictionary also describes a null game key for byes; absence of an entire row must still remain distinct from a verified bye. [NFL projection dictionary](https://sportsdata.io/developers/data-dictionary/nfl), [provider's Discovery Lab dictionary](https://discoverylab.sportsdata.io/developers/data-dictionary/nfl).

Historical warning is unusually explicit: the archive keeps final recorded versions, not correction trails, prior versions, or as-of queries. Historical injury records represent game-start status; intermediate practice reports and historical depth charts are not retained. Therefore purchase of history cannot reconstruct what a Tuesday trade model actually knew. Retain our own authorized, immutable offer-time snapshots from the start. [Historical integration guide](https://sportsdata.io/help/historical-data-integration-guide).

Commercial use is separately licensed and quoted by feed/use case. Discovery Lab is personal/noncommercial, not a route to a public business deployment. Request rights for raw caching, transformed scores, historical evaluation and post-termination evidence retention. [Data rights and licensing](https://sportsdata.io/help/data-rights-and-licensing-questions).

Open questions: whether future weeks beyond the upcoming game are populated with genuine current forecasts; supported IDP subcategories/position changes; inactive-player row conventions; forecast conditionality; and contractual rates/availability. Do not manufacture ROS by subtracting actuals from its frozen preseason forecast, or divide that forecast by 17.

## 3. RotoWire: explicit ROS semantics, incomplete public detail

The official API separates `WeeklyProjections.php` (week argument), `SeasonProjections.php` (full-season projections, including a free-agent collection), and `RestOfSeasonProjections.php` (current week/season and **remaining-season** stat projections for active players). The latter includes native player IDs and position-dependent projection objects. That is stronger horizon evidence than a generically named season endpoint, but “active players” leaves injured-reserve/returning-player coverage to verify. [Weekly contract](https://rotowire.readme.io/reference/get_football-nfl-weeklyprojections-php), [season contract](https://rotowire.readme.io/reference/get_football-nfl-seasonprojections-php), [ROS contract](https://rotowire.readme.io/reference/get_football-nfl-restofseasonprojections-php).

The consumer projection page exposes offense, K, IDP and team-defense categories and says forecasts are updated daily with additional news/inactive adjustments. This is not proof that the contracted API includes every category, all future weeks, a specific custom-scoring format, or a guaranteed update latency. [Projection coverage/update description](https://www.rotowire.com/football/projections.php?plp=1). Its API guide describes polling and recommends daily/lineup-triggered projection refresh, not a streaming feed. [Polling guidance](https://rotowire.readme.io/docs/polling-best-practices).

A naming trap: `WeeklyPoints.php`, despite being listed under the projection documentation family, is explicitly **actual** fantasy points scored. Do not ingest it as a forecast. [Actual weekly-points contract](https://rotowire.readme.io/reference/get_football-nfl-weeklypoints-php).

RotoWire offers business syndication, but its public consumer terms restrict automated extraction, commercial derived use and AI-related use absent authorization. A consumer membership is not the required license; seek a written feed agreement covering Fleeced's precise processing and audit storage. No data feed was accessed here. [Business syndication](https://www.rotowire.com/partner/), [terms, Articles VI–VII](https://www.rotowire.com/termsandconditions.php).

Open questions: complete raw-stat schema for weekly/ROS IDP, TEP/custom scoring inputs, exact current-week boundary, whether remaining totals already discount injuries/byes, native ID crosswalk completeness, row-level provider timestamps, historical forecast vintages, rate limits and retention rights. The public weekly page did not expose a full stat response schema; these remain contract/sample-validation requirements.

## 4. Public maintained projects: useful infrastructure, not a licensing shortcut

`nflreadr::load_ff_rankings()` reads DynastyProcess's FantasyPros ECR data, with draft/week/archive modes and documented weekly updates. Its dictionary includes scrape date, ECR dispersion and ranking type; those are not point forecasts or per-expert as-of guarantees. The repository identifies GPL-3.0 licensing and automated maintenance. Review applicable project and upstream terms separately rather than treating an open repository as a commercial grant from FantasyPros. [Loader](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_ff_rankings.html), [maintained data repository](https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data).

The crosswalk contains MFL, Sleeper, FantasyPros, RotoWire, GSIS, ESPN, Yahoo and FantasyData identifiers. It is useful for deterministic joins, with quarantine for missing/ambiguous mappings; it is not proof of complete coverage. Importantly, `sportsdata_id` in the ECR data denotes the **Sportradar UUID**, not SportsDataIO's numeric identifier. Preserve provider namespaces and never join those fields by similar spelling. [Player-ID dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_ff_playerids.html), [ECR dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_ff_rankings.html).

`ffpros` provides a projection-page scraper and metadata controls under an MIT code license. That license does not establish rights to the source data; it also does not provide a provider SLA. Prefer an authorized feed over building a new scraping dependency. [Projection scraper reference](https://ffpros.ffverse.com/reference/fp_projections.html), [code license](https://ffpros.ffverse.com/LICENSE.html).

`ffopportunity` estimates points from observed play-by-play opportunities. It is useful for historical features/validation, **not** a supplied forecast of future player opportunities. `ffsimulator` separately bootstraps player-week outcomes from ranks and historical outcomes: that is a model-generated prior requiring independent calibration, not a new observed provider projection. [Expected-opportunity methodology](https://ffopportunity.ffverse.com/), [simulation methodology](https://ffsimulator.ffverse.com/reference/ffs_generate_projections).

## Format and evidence acceptance requirements

The following are engineering recommendations/inferences, not vendor capabilities asserted without verification:

- **Scoring:** request raw expected stat counts. Linear scoring can apply PPR/half-PPR, passing-TD rules and position-specific reception premiums such as TEP. Do not compute threshold bonuses from mean yardage: a bonus on a mean is not the mean bonus. Unsupported categories remain unsupported, not zero.
- **SF/2QB:** use real position eligibility and legal slot optimization; do not boost the QB's football stat forecast. IDP needs exact tackle/sack/turnover components and league position mappings; D/ST is not an IDP substitute.
- **Horizon:** weekly, preseason full-season and remaining-season are separate types. Require explicit included games/weeks, current-week handling and fantasy-season cutoff. A ROS total alone cannot reveal week-specific bye overlap or replacement-lineup gains.
- **Availability:** require whether projections are conditional on playing or already include missed-game probability. Do not discount twice. Preserve observed zero, confirmed bye/out, not-projected, stale and ID-unmapped as different states. A player's bye may be zero; the manager's legal replacement slot need not be.
- **IDs/coverage:** sample deep benches, rookies, free agents, returning IR players and each supported defensive position, for both teams. Exact provider crosswalk plus version; no silent fuzzy-name match. Draft picks stay future/dynasty assets, not fabricated weekly points.
- **As-of:** negotiate retention rights, then freeze provider/model/version, source timestamp, fetched-at time, horizon, stat units, scoring interpretation, coverage manifest and content hash. Historical final values cannot certify past knowledge. Retain original evidence when providers revise data.
- **Qualification:** after explicit license approval, validate a bounded sample and shadow weekly lineup deltas before season/ROS claims. Evaluate calibration/error and missingness by position, horizon and scoring regime against future held-out outcomes. Do not assume providers are independent or that an average of them improves accuracy.

Parent handoff: pursue licensed weekly raw stats first; keep a rank-only fallback visibly ordinal/Unknown for numeric starter points. RotoWire merits a conditional ROS contract/sample review; SportsDataIO merits a conditional weekly/IDP review; FantasyPros merits review only after competition/use permission is clear. No reviewed source is yet authorized, integrated, payload-qualified or shown empirically superior for Fleeced by this research.
