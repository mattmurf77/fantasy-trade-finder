Fantasy Football Projection Capability Specification
Product and engineering implementation brief
Version 1 | November 7 2026 | Proposed capability, not implemented

1 Purpose and product decisions
Build an app capability that answers two connected questions: how likely is each team to make the playoffs or earn a bye, and where will each non-playoff team’s original draft pick land? Show final-record distributions, remaining matchup odds, projected Max Points For, and current pick ownership. The primary users are fantasy managers evaluating their season and dynasty picks, and commissioners explaining league outcomes.
The initial integration is Sleeper NFL. This is an advisory, read-only feature. It does not change lineups, make trades, pick up free agents, set league draft order, or place bets. It should fit the existing app’s authentication, navigation, design, and backend rather than assume a new application. The confirmed target is the existing Fantasy Trade Finder (Fleeced) app and repository in the confirmed local checkout.
MVP supports the demonstrated league class: head-to-head regular season, one matchup per team per week, configurable playoff field and byes, no divisions or median match, and non-playoff draft order ascending by regular-season Max PF. Support custom scoring, IDP, flexible positions, ties, and traded picks. Detect unsupported seeding formats and require a supported configured policy; never silently flatten divisions or ignore median wins. Later phases add these formats, player-level joint simulations, scenarios, and playoff-team draft ordering.

2 Confirmed user requirements and reference league
The user wants actual league data fetched directly, not manually re-entered screenshots. Use season scoring averages alongside optimized future lineups because currently set lineups often leave bye players in place. Return playoff probability, bye probability and final-record projections for every team. For an IDP position lacking bye coverage, assume a free-agent pickup scoring the league average for that starting position. Apply this only to an uncovered bye slot, not arbitrary upgrades, injury-only holes or offense/kicker holes by default.
For non-playoff draft order, use the official standings Max PF and its average per completed week instead of actual PF. The lowest final Max PF among the non-playoff teams receives the earliest pick. Determine non-playoff membership from simulated actual matchup results, not from Max PF standings. Keep original team and current pick owner separate.
Sanitized reference fixture: Sleeper league REFERENCE_LEAGUE_ID, private reference league, 2026 NFL season; 12 teams, 6 playoff spots, 2 first-round byes, playoffs begin Week 15. Scoring includes half-PPR and custom IDP rules. Fifteen starting slots: QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, K, DL, DL, LB, LB, DB, DB, IDP FLEX. The baseline snapshot has eight completed regular-season weeks and six remaining weeks. These values are fixture data, not production constants.

3 Terminology and output semantics
Actual PF is the sum of scores from the lineup used for real matchups, including applicable adjustments. Official Max PF is the provider’s aggregate of hindsight-best eligible lineups. Optimized projected lineup is the highest expected score from eligible players before outcomes are known; it is not the same as hindsight Max PF. Mathematically, the expected maximum of realized scores generally exceeds the maximum of expected scores.
A projected final record can mean an average record or the most frequent exact record. Show both with labels. Expected wins need not be integers; the modal record is not a rounded expected record. Independently modal team records need not represent one jointly possible season. Store wins, losses and ties separately, and optionally show win equivalents as wins plus half of ties. Do not infer clinching from a rounded 100% simulation result.
P(pick k) is unconditional and includes the risk that the original team reaches the playoffs. Expected non-playoff pick is conditional on missing playoffs. Show no conditional value when fewer than 100 qualifying simulations exist, a proposed stability threshold. Pick probabilities across non-playoff slots must sum to that team’s missed-playoff probability, not 100%. A current owner can have multiple picks; expected count at each slot is distinct from the probability of owning at least one pick in a range.

4 User experience
Entry: open a league and select Projections, or provide Sleeper username/league ID, select league and season, then run. Show the verified league name, last completed week, playoff format, draft-order rule and last successful data snapshot. Username discovery is convenience only, not permission to mutate league data.
Playoff view: all teams, current W-L-T, PF, PPG, expected final W-L-T, modal final record, playoff odds and bye odds. Sort by playoff odds by default and allow team/name/record sorting. A team detail view shows remaining opponent, projected score distribution, matchup win/tie/loss probabilities, optimal expected lineup, locked actuals, excluded players, assumed pickups and gaps. Separate true win probability from win-equivalent probability.
Draft view: original team, current owner, current Max PF, Max PF/week, projected final Max PF, non-playoff probability, probabilities of picks 1 through N, and expected pick conditional on missing. The default sort is projected Max PF ascending, explicitly labeled; this is not a deterministic final draft order. Offer a compact mobile summary and horizontally scrollable probability grid. Display current owner only for the selected year/round. Do not infer later rounds from first-round ownership.
Explain controls: model assumptions, data coverage, historical weight, number of simulations, scoring rules, roster policy, tie policy, last update and model version. An advanced control can compare 40%, 60% and 80% historical weighting. All scenario output carries a scenario label and must not overwrite the baseline. Empty slots and hypothetical FA contributions must be visible, including the distinction between a zero projection component and historical blending that still cushions a gap.
States: loading, queued, running, complete, stale, incomplete projections, unsupported league policy and failed. Continue showing the last valid run with its timestamp when refresh fails. Never replace failed output with zeros or silently drop teams. No auto-refresh promise or notification is required for MVP; offer a user-triggered refresh.

5 Data acquisition and canonical model
Use server-side provider adapters and immutable snapshots. Read Sleeper NFL state, league settings, users, rosters, weekly matchups, player metadata, historical scoring, projections and traded picks. Official API aggregate PF and Max PF are authoritative for totals. Parse split integer/decimal fields with decimal arithmetic, preserving signed values correctly, and verify provider field meanings against fixtures rather than assuming a key exists. Retrieve all completed regular-season matchup weeks and remaining schedule weeks through playoff_start minus one.
Public endpoints include /v1/state/nfl, /v1/user/{username}, /v1/user/{user_id}/leagues/nfl/{season}, /v1/league/{id}, /rosters, /users, /matchups/{week}, /traded_picks and /v1/players/nfl. Projection and live-stat routes used by the prototype are /projections/nfl/{season}/{week}?season_type=regular and /stats/nfl/{season}/{week}?season_type=regular. Treat these latter routes as replaceable, potentially unsupported provider dependencies. Verify production usage rights, attribution, terms, rate limits and provider reliability before launch; do not claim an official supported projections API.
Store LeagueSnapshot, TeamSnapshot, PlayerWeekInput, ScheduleMatchup, RosterEligibility, ProjectionCoverage, PickOwnership, ModelConfig and SimulationRun. Every record needs provider identifiers, season/week, fetched_at and source version/hash. Team identity is roster_id, not mutable team name. Pick identity is season, round and original roster_id; owner_roster_id is mutable. Preserve historical snapshots for reproducibility without retaining unnecessary personal data.
A run stores input hash, model version, scoring-rules hash, settings, random seed, iterations, status, warnings and outputs. Sources fetched at different times must be checked for consistency. If a week finalizes during ingestion, restart or freeze a consistent snapshot. Never add in-progress points twice when aggregate standings already include them. Stop on mismatched PF/records unless a documented commissioner adjustment explains the difference.

6 Scoring and lineup optimization
Translate all supported league scoring rules into canonical stat calculations. Use raw projected stats, not provider generic PPR totals that can omit IDP or bonuses. Reject unsupported nonlinear bonuses unless a compatible projection distribution or documented approximation is available. Missing stats are unknown, not always zero. A known zero projection, no scheduled game and a missing projection are different states.
Solve a maximum-weight eligible assignment: each slot receives at most one player, each player occupies at most one slot, and all locked players stay fixed for actual matchups. Handle dual positions and overlapping FLEX/SUPER_FLEX/IDP slots through provider eligibility. Empty slots are explicit candidates when legal; do not indiscriminately discard negative projections or clamp negative scores. League scoring can be negative. Whether a manager is modeled to leave a slot intentionally empty must be an explicit lineup policy.
For actual matchup forecasts, use active starters/bench and current-week availability. Current-week games already started preserve actual locked starters; benched locked players cannot be inserted retroactively. Use NFL kickoff and live status, not date-string comparisons. Current in-game points and remaining projection are separate. If remaining-game projection is unavailable, label the live estimate approximate or support pregame/completed-games-only mode rather than treating a partial score as final.
For future weeks, distinguish projected return dates, unknown injuries, reserve activation and taxi promotion. The prototype excluded reserves/taxi from actual lineup forecasts, but the Max PF report included projected reserve returns. MVP must expose and version these policies rather than apply inconsistent hidden pools. For Max PF, include all provider-eligible roster categories, including taxi per Sleeper guidance; IR/reserve treatment requires an official rule or validated fixture before asserting exact replication. Do not assume current rosters reproduce historical ownership.
IDP bye replacements: calculate mean actual score per occupied eligible starting-position slot across completed weeks, including zero performances. Exclude unoccupied slots from the denominator; identify manual adjustments separately. Use DL, LB, DB and IDP-flex averages independently. Permit an assumed FA only when a counterfactual eligibility check confirms a bye-induced deficiency after optimizing real roster coverage. An unrelated player on bye cannot authorize filling an injury gap. Never replace a healthy available starter merely because the FA average is higher. Use distinct virtual players to prevent reusing one replacement twice. A real pickup is never executed. Mark these as league-starter-average assumptions, not measured available-waiver averages. Do not put hypothetical pickups into official historical Max PF.

7 Baseline playoff simulation
MVP preserves the requested baseline while exposing its limitations. Let H_i be completed regular-season actual PF divided by completed scoring weeks and O_iw the optimized expected weekly lineup score. Default full-week mean M_iw = 0.60 H_i + 0.40 O_iw. Configure the weight per run. Count team scoring weeks, not number of head-to-head contests; formats with extra median games must not double the denominator.
For each simulated season draw persistent team-strength uncertainty and weekly score shocks. The inspected prototype smooths each team’s observed weekly variance 50/50 toward pooled within-team variance and uses a 5-point normal persistent team shock. Those are reference parameters, not calibrated truths. Version them, validate their scale across different scoring systems, and widen or flag uncertainty for short histories. In Week 1, use an explicit projection-only prior with league variance priors; never divide by zero.
The prototype’s partial-week approximation is locked actuals plus historical weight times max(0, H_i minus pregame projections for locked starters) plus projection weight times remaining optimized score; uncertainty scales with the unplayed slot share. Retain only as an explicitly labeled compatibility mode. Production preferred mode removes the locked share from both the mean and variance using remaining player-level contribution estimates, adds actuals exactly once, and allows negative realized outcomes when rules permit. Test that the historical term does not predict points from already completed slots a second time.
Simulate paired matchup scores once, award wins/losses/ties at league precision, accumulate actual PF and PA from those same scores, and rank with the validated league seeding policy. The fixture uses win percentage/win equivalents, PF and higher PA, then randomized unresolved exact ties. These tie rules are provider/league-specific and must be verified or commissioner-confirmed, not universal defaults. Top P teams qualify and top B receive byes. Keep future matchup schedule fixed within the baseline.
Run 250,000 scenarios for the reference full run with deterministic seed and chunking. A smaller preview can be labeled provisional. Report Monte Carlo sampling error separately from model uncertainty; more runs do not cure biased inputs. No odds should imply backtested calibration until evaluation supports that claim.

8 Joint Max PF and draft simulation
Start with official completed-week Max PF A_i and Hmax_i = A_i / completed scoring weeks. Baseline future weekly Max PF mean is 0.60 Hmax_i + 0.40 Omax_iw, where the optimization pool follows Max PF eligibility rather than actual-starting-lineup locks. Completed-game scores for benched players can enter a hindsight best lineup. Max PF ends at the league’s regular-season boundary unless a different draft rule is explicitly selected.
The baseline is an approximation because projected optimum understates expected hindsight optimum. Show that caveat. Preferred upgraded engine samples coherent player stat/point outcomes, optimizes each simulated hindsight lineup, and calibrates projected distributions against historical lineup depth and Max PF. Do not simply add an unvalidated fixed optimism factor.
Actual score and Max PF must be simulated jointly. For the compatibility model, use correlated weekly residuals; the prior draft report estimated correlation 0.818 from reconstructed history and enforced Max PF at least actual lineup score. This coefficient is fixture-specific, not a global constant. The bound only applies when score definitions, adjustments and eligible pools match. A flooring correction changes expected means, so compute displayed projected final Max PF from realized simulation output, not merely the pre-floor formula. Prefer nonnegative optimization-surplus modeling or player-level joint simulation for the production upgrade.
In each same simulated season, first identify playoff/non-playoff membership using actual matchup scores. Then sort only non-playoff teams by final Max PF ascending and assign picks 1 through N. This preserves correlation between making playoffs and scoring potential. Do not combine independently sampled playoff flags with independently sampled draft ranks. Unknown Max PF tie rules must be configured or labeled; randomized ties are an explicit fallback scenario, not a statement of league rules.
Return unconditional slot probabilities, conditional slot probabilities when useful, conditional expected pick, Max PF mean/quantiles and missed-playoff probability. Map original picks to current owners after ranking. If ownership changes, rerender ownership from a new snapshot without changing original-team finish odds, unless rosters also changed. Picks 7 onward stay unprojected until the playoff draft-order policy is supplied. Do not label a projected non-playoff rank as the full draft order.

9 Architecture and service contracts
Use provider adapters, snapshot storage, a scoring engine, a constrained lineup optimizer, a deterministic simulation service, an asynchronous job queue and result storage. Keep provider fetching out of simulation loops. Cache metadata separately from weekly projections and live scoring; use conservative backoff and provider-respecting concurrency. Proposed freshness targets are 24 hours for metadata, 6 hours for future projections, and 5 minutes for live inputs only where supported. Display actual timestamps, not guarantees.
Proposed API: POST /leagues/{id}/projection-runs with season, snapshot policy, model config and optional scenario ID returns run_id, status and input hash. GET /projection-runs/{run_id} returns status, progress, warnings and result URLs. GET /projection-runs/{run_id}/teams returns playoff/bye/record metrics; /draft returns original-team/owner/slot distributions; /teams/{roster_id}/weeks returns lineup and matchup explanations. POST must be idempotent for an unchanged input/config/version hash; repeated refreshes reuse compatible runs. Cancel jobs safely without publishing partial results as complete.
Numeric results use 0–1 probabilities, explicit null for unavailable metrics and decimal-safe scores. Include sample count and qualifying sample count for conditional metrics. Export CSV plus a machine-readable run manifest with all assumptions. Avoid returning raw unrelated user profile data. Validate IDs and inputs, authorize access to saved app data, bound simulation counts and payload sizes, and prevent provider calls from becoming arbitrary URL fetches.
Proposed operational targets, subject to load testing: cached result p95 under 2 seconds; reference 12-team six-week full run p95 under 60 seconds excluding provider delay; asynchronous progress immediately. The player-level upgrade may require fewer online samples plus cached full runs. Track provider latency, coverage, queue time, optimizer failures, reconciliation errors, run duration and schema drift without logging unnecessary private roster owner details.

10 Validation and acceptance criteria
Data: fixture has exactly 12 rosters and 36 remaining head-to-head contests. Official historical PF and W-L-T reconcile, or produce a blocking warning. Max PF aggregate is copied exactly, while reconstructed history is only a variance input. Historical reconstruction discrepancies must be quantified; the prior report had four totals differing by 1 to 11.6 points, so it must not be called exact.
Optimization: every selected player is eligible, no duplicate player appears, locked actual starters cannot move, dual-position assignment matches a brute-force oracle on small cases, and byes/no-game states are not confused with missing inputs. A DL bye plus an unrelated DB injury cannot create a DB FA replacement. A healthy bench cover prevents an unnecessary virtual pickup. A high-scoring benched Thursday player is excluded from actual lineup changes but considered for Max PF.
Simulation invariants: every game yields one win and one loss or two team ties; total win equivalents equal scheduled games plus historical equivalents. Playoff probabilities sum to P and bye probabilities to B. Every non-playoff pick column sums to 1. Each team’s non-playoff pick row sums to its missed-playoff probability. Conditional probabilities sum to 1 only when the conditioning event has enough samples. Owner pick counts reconcile with ownership records and do not overwrite origin IDs.
Edge cases: negative player points, fractional scoring ties, empty slot, all players on bye, no history, short history, stale projection, missing IDP stat, nonlinear bonus, midweek roster trade, postponed game, stat correction, owner/team-name change, manual standings adjustment, playoff-start change, unknown draft tie policy, no non-playoff teams and already-finished regular season. Unsupported divisions/median/manual seeding block confident output rather than silently approximating.
Regression: freeze the inspected November 6 fixture and seed. Compatibility playoff results should reproduce the saved outputs within floating-point tolerance if code/inputs remain identical; use statistical tolerance when the algorithm intentionally changes and record why. Draft regression requires the original missing draft inputs/package to be restored; the report alone is not a reproducible fixture. Do not force a corrected model to match a flawed baseline.
Calibration: rolling historical backtests must use only information available at each forecast date. Measure matchup and playoff Brier score/log loss, probability reliability, final-win error, Max PF error and interval coverage, draft-slot calibration and rank error. Compare against PPG-only, projections-only and historical-Max-PF-only baselines. Publish beta/model-estimate labeling until performance and coverage are reviewed. Establish launch thresholds with the product owner using held-out leagues, not fabricated accuracy claims.

11 Delivery plan and decisions
Phase 1: normalize Sleeper inputs and rule coverage, verify scoring/Max PF policy, implement immutable fixtures and audit UI. Phase 2: implement baseline optimizer and actual-score simulation, final-record distribution and weekly explanations. Phase 3: implement joint Max PF simulation, non-playoff slot probabilities and traded-pick ownership. Phase 4: complete UI, exports, failure states, reproducibility and rolling backtests. Phase 5: expand league formats and player-level joint scoring after validation.
Launch gate: all deterministic invariants pass, unsupported formats are blocked, rule choices are visible, missing data cannot masquerade as zero, snapshots reproduce results, mobile tables remain usable, and users can explain why their projected pick is conditional. Probability model quality is a separate gate from computational correctness.
Open decisions requiring product confirmation: supported leagues at launch; source/licensing for multiweek projections; official IR/reserve Max PF eligibility; default reserve activation assumptions; injury-only and offensive FA policy; commissioner manual-seeding support; exact Max PF tie rule; playoff-team draft ordering; refresh frequency; retention and sharing policy. Reasonable defaults in this document are proposals, not new user instructions.
