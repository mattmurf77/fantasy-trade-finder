## Scoring normalization and explicit approximation

Sleeper can retain kicker/team-defense coefficients in offense-only leagues. Finite known K or team-defense coefficients are removed only when the corresponding active K or DEF/DST/D/ST slot is absent. This does not enable active K/DEF/IDP lineups. Unknown scoring rules are not discarded by prefix matching. Original `source_scoring_settings` remain in the immutable league/snapshot identity alongside effective rules.

A separate, explicit beta approximation omits nonzero finite coefficients for exactly `st_ff`, `st_fum_rec`, `st_td` and `fum_rec_td` when the forecast provider does not declare support. These offensive-player special-teams/fumble events are not unused K/DEF rules and are not forecast as zero events. The exact excluded coefficients are retained in metadata `scoring_exclusions`; the warning is **“Rare special-teams/fumble bonuses are not projected.”** Provider-supported keys remain included. Other unsupported rules and invalid numeric values still fail validation. [Implementation detail](../../plans/win-now/BUILD.md#release-scoring-boundaries--2026-09-05).

## Worker behavior during deployment

The build-time player-cache import sets `FTF_BUILD_MODE=1`, suppressing Win Now startup. Rolling containers never requeue another container's unexpired running job. Queued jobs resume; expired work fails, and late completion cannot revive terminal/deleted/expired work. After a worker crash, its running search remains unavailable until its original 15-minute expiry, then the user can retry. Automatic ownership/heartbeat-based retries are not implemented.
