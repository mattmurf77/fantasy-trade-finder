# Business context

Role skills use this page for business objectives and source links. [Product overview](../product/overview.md) owns the product description; [HANDOFF](../../living-memory/HANDOFF.md) links current release evidence. Do not maintain separate version, platform, or test-count snapshots here.

## Business goal

Build a sustainable dynasty football product, with subscriptions and/or ads among the recorded monetization candidates. Decisions remain in the [monetization plans](../plans/monetization/README.md); those plans do not establish revenue, SDK integration, or an approved commercial launch. Confirm live commercial status and costs with the operator's records before a financial claim.

The operator owns product/release decisions. Strategy work can propose pricing, acquisition, retention, partnerships, and distribution, while build work follows [agent workflow](../agent-workflow.md).

## Measurement

The adopted north star is **WAT (Weekly Active Traders)** and the funnel definitions are in the [analytics program](analytics/2026-07-17-analytics-program-plan.md) and [tracking plan](analytics/2026-07-17-tracking-plan-v2.md). Instrumentation exists in backend and clients; inspect the actual taxonomy/emitters and observed rows before claiming coverage or outcomes. A registered name does not prove events are emitted, retained, or attributable.

Measure useful proposals and trades, with exposure and identity context. If a role needs unavailable data, specify the instrumentation needed rather than invent counts. The [analytics initiative](../plans/analytics-platform/README.md) records design history; code and measured data establish the implemented/live behavior.

## Market and costs

Dynasty attention changes across rookie drafts, training camp, and the NFL season. Use dated competitor evidence and distinguish observations from assumptions. The product's recorded differentiator is personal valuations plus actionable discovery, rather than one universal consensus ranking.

Cost categories include Render/database hosting, Apple distribution, EAS builds, model API usage, and domains. Current fees, contracts, renewals, and marketing spend belong in financial/operator records; old estimates are not a current budget. See [dependency/source map](../product/dependencies-and-sources.md).
