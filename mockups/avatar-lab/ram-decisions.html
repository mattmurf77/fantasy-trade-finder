1 · Settled

D-155 · 2026-08-22

The ram is the mascot and the guide avatar. The fumbling-running-back concept is retired unbuilt — the shipped icon had already contradicted it. Q-009 closed after 15 months as “neither — the ram”. Six docs updated, no code touched.

Named Fleeced, after the product. Deliberately unresolved: “I'm Fleeced” as the bubble's opening line reads as “I got ripped off”, so which of the six “The Analyst” strings take the character name is deferred to build time. Analytics unaffected — naming changes copy, not the pose values.

D-155 · raster exception, scoped

Mascot may ship as raster sprites — @2x/@3x, alpha, ≤ 60 KB, only under mascot/ram/. Vector remains the rule everywhere else. “No gradient, no glow” still governs UI chrome; the mascot is exempt inside its own box only.

D2 · the bubble anchor moves off-centre

BUBBLE_ANCHOR becomes per-mascot — the horns are the silhouette, and a ram with horns low enough to clear top-centre stops reading as a ram. Open: which corner. Top-left is likely, since point aims right and would otherwise point into its own bubble.

Art direction · three corrections you made
  • One pose at a time — six-up sheets lost identity every time.
  • Keep the smug, menacing look — this reversed an error of mine; the icon glares on purpose.
  • Solid neon-pink eyes, no pupils, stronger glow.

2 · Medium — painted, everywhere

D-156 · 2026-08-23

Fleeced ships painted in every pose, at every size, on every surface. The per-pose painted/flat mapping was explored and resolved the simpler way. D-155's raster exception is therefore used, and the flat exploration is retired to reference art in ram-art/poses-flat/.

This is chosen against the 44 pt evidence, deliberately. Painted expressions collapse at 44 pt where flat held their read. That is a known, accepted cost — recorded so nobody later “fixes” it as an oversight.

3 · Sizing — matched to the Analyst's ink box

size is the rendered width of the box, not the character, and the shipped Analyst never fills its box. Measured with getBBox, its ink occupies 62–90 pt of a 96 pt box, with about a third of the height empty above neutral's head. The first ram sprites were trimmed and padded square, so they filled ~100% and rendered visibly oversized.

The rule

Mascot art is inset so its ink occupies 70% of the box width, centred — matching neutral (67%) and the median of the six. Fleeced now measures 66.6 pt wide at size=96, inside the Analyst's own 62–90 pt range. Full-fill originals are kept at poses-alpha-full/, so it is reversible. A side effect: sprites shrank, worst @3x 24.9 KB → 15.6 KB, now 3.8× inside budget.

The ram renders taller than the Analyst, and that is sanctioned. Analyst viewBoxes are 150–170 × 150, so height is size × 0.88–1.00; the ram's sprites are square, so height is size. Each pair below shares one size value and sits on a common baseline.

Cost, recorded so the trade is explicit

The entire shipped Analyst — six poses, shared part-kit, switcher — is 15.3 KB of react-native-svg: less than one painted ram sprite at @3x, and about 1/12th of the painted set after the inset. Vector costs the same at every size; raster pays three times over. This did not change the decision.
