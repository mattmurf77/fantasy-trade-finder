Trade suggestion redesign

The presentation rebuild (build item 3): replaces the flat scrollable list with a pyramid — one endorsed hero → a small Featured tier → an uncapped, ranked "All trades" browse list (views and dismissals are ranking-training signal), adds reciprocal (two-sided) explanations on the costly action, MESO shape variants, banded confidence capped by data volume, a turn-state machine for trade threads, a double-opt-in mutual reveal, an honest empty state with price feedback, and a disciplined push channel. Chalkline tokens throughout; every screen is a 390px phone frame with a one-line research caption.

The nine states
01
Today's Trade (hero)
One endorsed suggestion: binary badge, asymmetric two-sided explanation, fairness as a league-normal range band.
Kleinerman 2018: reciprocal explanation +17pp acceptance · Hinge 8×
02
MESO variant picker
Same centerpiece, three equivalent-value return shapes: youth / picks / consolidation. The pick is preference elicitation.
Ames & Mason 2015: higher acceptance, satisfaction, anchoring · cap 3
03
Featured (the bench)
Small Featured tier — 5 compact rows, each a distinct partner and shape — with "Browse all N trades" into the full list.
Choice overload → rejection mind-set (Pronk 2020) · small at the top of the funnel
04
Confidence states
Three labeled bands, no percentages; ceiling capped by data volume with a "rank 10 more players" pivot.
OkCupid lower-bound rule · miscalibration kills trust (Li 2024)
05
Turn states + threads
your_move / their_move / stalled(3d) / expiring / ended chips; your-move sorts top; expiry warning + extend; one-tap polite withdraw.
Hinge Your Turn: −25% dead matches · cap open loops
06
Mutual-interest reveal
Double opt-in sheet: "FTF found a fit between your boards," prefilled opening message into negotiation.
T1: symmetric provenance protects plausible deniability
07
Honest empty state
No viable trades → price-feedback pivot: your ask vs. league consensus, adjust or hold. No degraded filler.
Confidently-wrong is the worst error class in a repeated game
08
Push-notification copy
Three archetypes ≤10 words with names: gain-framed digest, loss-framed expiry, real-time state change; ≤3/week budget.
≤10 words ≈ 2× CTR · personalization ≈ 4× reaction
09
Browse all (uncapped)
Ranked, infinite "All trades" list under the Featured tier — per-row dismiss, dismissed-state acknowledgment, scroll-to-load.
Every view/dismiss is ranking-training signal · mine declines (IBM/ESPN 76.9→97.3%)

Design principles carried through every state
P1
Scarcity is the endorsement — not the catalog. One hero per league per cycle, binary badge, a small Featured tier — no endorsement ladders. Below that, the full ranked list stays uncapped and browsable, because every viewed and dismissed card is ranking-training signal.
P2
Asymmetric two-sided explanation. Your side: ≤3 concrete feature bullets. Their side: a confidence statement only — the other manager's board is never exposed, in any state.
P3
No winner needle, anywhere. Fairness renders as "within league-normal range" bands vs. public consensus. A winner meter becomes a league-chat weapon and trains users to demand a calc-win on every deal.
P4
Plausible deniability end-to-end. Passes are invisible, interest is revealed only after double opt-in, and provenance is always the app's ("FTF found a fit"), never a manager's request.
P5
Honesty compounds. Confidence is banded and capped by data volume; empty states name the blocking price instead of padding; expiry always warns and offers rescue before dropping.
