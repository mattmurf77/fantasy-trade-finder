Channel discipline
Budget ≤3 pushes/week/user, hard-capped — autothrottled
Timing Digest lands at the user's median historical open hour
Real-time Only two-party state changes: offer received, answered, expiring
Everything else Batches into the Tuesday digest — no stream
Rationale

≤10 words ≈ 2× the CTR of 11–20 (Localytics); personalization with names ≈ 4× reaction rate (Airship). Loss-frame the expiry ("expires tonight"), gain-frame the digest ("is ready"). Frequency is the channel-survival lever: 46% opt out at 2–5 pushes/week, so FTF hard-caps at 3 and batches the rest (Fitz 2019: scheduled batches beat streams; hourly ≈ no benefit). Per-user send timing beats any fixed hour by ~23–25%.
