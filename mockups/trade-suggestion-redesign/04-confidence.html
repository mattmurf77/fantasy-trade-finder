Rationale

OkCupid rule: displayed confidence is the lower bound given data volume — two shared questions can never show "94%." Three labeled bands, no percentages, at FTF's N (round-2 §5.3). The cap doubles as the data-collection incentive: "rank 10 more players" raises the ceiling.
