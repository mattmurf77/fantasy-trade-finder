#300 round 2 — League rankings → trade candidates

Second-round redesign lab for feedback #300, drawn against origin/main @ 53bd19f — i.e. against #299 as shipped, not against round 1’s mockup box model. Three standalone pages; open any of them directly.

No iframes on this page, by design. Round 1’s index did not render at all in Chrome over file://. This one is plain links and static HTML — nothing to fail. Each page below is fully self-contained (inline CSS, inline SVG, no scripts, no external fonts); the only external reference in the whole lab is one <img src> to the real screen capture at ../../screens/mobile/league-summary/populated.png, which resolves correctly from file:// because it is a relative path from a real file, not from srcdoc.
Pages

1 · directions.html — the two competing shapes →

Today’s screen filtered to WR; Direction 1 (operator’s: collapsed Trade-candidates strip on the shipped OutlookStrip pattern + Buyers/Sellers pills, with the drill-ins); Direction 2 (one ranked list, one median divider built from the shipped playoff-cutline construction, with the odd-team-count case and the honest cost of losing the intent toggle); the operator’s literal Buyers / Median / Sellers three-section phrasing drawn three ways (empty middle → invented tolerance band → the form that actually works); the shared drill-in; and the recommendation.

The shipped tile measured against real font metrics (name budget 143.8pt, worst-case tier badge 63.5pt); three ways to satisfy the 44pt touch minimum on a 32pt row, including why a nested control is clipped by overflow: 'hidden' and invisible to VoiceOver/Maestro; Variant D (drop the injury + rookie tags to buy a visible “Offer”) re-measured — it fits, with 2.6pt to spare; “Offer” vs “Target”; and the side-by-side question answered with numbers.

3 · values-and-states.html — pick tiers and the missing states →

The three value vocabularies already in the codebase and which one a median delta may use; the ladder audit that kills delta-as-pick-tier (4 of 12 deltas collapse onto “an Early 1st”; the biggest gap in the league has no name at all); the exact floor, read out of _pick_gap_equivalent; the new API field the divider needs; filter-changed-while-drilled-in; and the edge states nobody has drawn — multi-select, flat league, unpriceable rows.

measure-tile.py — the measurements, runnable

python3 measure-tile.py. Loads the same TTFs the app loads (@expo-google-fonts/{archivo,ibm-plex-mono} from mobile/node_modules), sums real hmtx advance widths with React Native’s letterSpacing, and adds the exact paddings and borders from PlayerCard.tsx and components/chalkline/Badge.tsx. Every pt figure in this lab comes from it.

Direction 1 adds a persisted collapse state, a segmented control and a second list whose every row already appears below it, and buys one saved scroll flick.
