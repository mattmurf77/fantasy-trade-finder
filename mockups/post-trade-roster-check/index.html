Design notes / separate from app copy

A receipt, not a second verdict

The only proposed mount is the disclosure after CardImpactBlock (or after the calculator’s existing lineup table). No new value bar, “why you / why them” section, odds model, trade action, or modal. The starter movement above remains the source of the comparison.

Coverage has a narrow meaning

Count supported offensive slots and unique bench players after the complete trade. A player used at FLEX is not a backup. A lower-value replacement can fill a slot; keep that quality loss visible. Bench eligibility does not establish weekly availability or simultaneous injury cover.

Estimates never earn a green check

Current ESPN/MFL/Fleaflicker slot resolution uses a standardized template. The incomplete example labels every count as provisional, withholds confirmed coverage, and identifies unresolved roster entries. Unknown never becomes zero.
