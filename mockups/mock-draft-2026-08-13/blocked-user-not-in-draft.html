Rationale

  • Placement in the ladder: the arm sits after class_not_loaded — with the server-answered refusals, ahead of the board-derived ones — keyed on postRefusal === 'user_not_in_draft' || probeReason === 'user_not_in_draft' (LLD §3.3). Drawing it as a muted card (not an error card) matches its nature: a league condition, not a failure.
  • The dead CTA states the condition. House rule from the six shipped arms: the button label never says “Start” and never lies about a tap doing something. “Your team isn't in this draft” follows “Needs 6+ teams” / “Not during a live draft” grammar.
  • Why both surfaces: the entry card covers the normal path (probe answers before any tap); the session-screen arm covers arrivals that bypass the room (deep link, back-stack resume, a mock that refused at create via the engine raise). Both speak the one reason vocabulary — the user never learns which enforcement point fired (HLD §3.3).
  • Honesty floor: neither surface suggests when or whether this resolves — that depends on the league's platform data, which the app can't promise. The copy names the fact and stops.
