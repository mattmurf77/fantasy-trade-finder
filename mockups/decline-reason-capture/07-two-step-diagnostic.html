Two-layer decline capture

There is no ✕. One button means interested; layer 1 is the pass — three tiles, one row. Layer 2 opens beneath them only once a tile is picked, and choosing it moves straight to the next trade.

Layer 1 is banked the instant it is tapped, so a tester who stops there still leaves a complete row: passed, on value. Layer 2 sharpens it; it never gates it.

Two layers, three doors

Layer 1 — the pass. Three tiles across one row: Value, Fit, Neither. Tapping one records the disposition and the reason in a single gesture, so a pass cannot exist without a reason and a reason cannot exist without a pass.

Layer 2 — the specific. Opens full-width beneath the tiles, notched to the one that opened it, never as a separate screen: two options under Value, three under Fit, a text box under Neither. Picking one writes and advances.

Tapping a different tile swaps which layer 2 is open — the first answer stays recorded, so switching is a refinement rather than a reset. Tapping the open tile collapses it.
