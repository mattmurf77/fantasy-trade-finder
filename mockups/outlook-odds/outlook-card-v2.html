Trade Outlook Card v2 — Playoff-Only

The July-approved outlook-card.html paired playoff% and title% as its central two-up rhythm and closed with a 2026/27/28 championship-odds block. Title odds have no demonstrated skill (CI spans zero; 3 of 6 leagues worse than guessing) and multi-year odds compound every unvalidated dependency — both are removed, not caveated. The design problem: keep the card whole. The answer: projected record takes title's cell — the two-up rhythm survives with two validated outputs (record | playoff odds), and the multi-year block is replaced by a scope label ("2026 season").

C · Weeks 0–5 — no odds block at all recommended

While meta.beta is true a trade's odds delta is the difference of two numbers the model hasn't earned — and in bands, most trades read "Toss-up → Toss-up". The card is already complete without it: value verdict + the SHIPPED position-impact tier chips (#169/#238) carry "why this trade".
