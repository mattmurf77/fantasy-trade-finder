League Summary — Outlook v2

The one surface that earned full odds treatment. The shipped OddsSection (dark, flag outlook.odds) is three revisions behind the calibration evidence: it renders title odds (no demonstrated skill — removed, not caveated), raw 1%-precision percentages at any week (bands are the verdict for weeks 0–5), and a decimal "proj seed 3.2". The v2 merges projected standings (the list order + projected record — previously shown nowhere) and playoff odds (the band chip) into one seed-ordered section keyed to the existing meta.beta signal, which clears at completed_weeks ≥ 6 — the calibrated gate, no new mechanism.
