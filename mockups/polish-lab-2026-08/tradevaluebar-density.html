Polish Lab · TradeValueBar Density — #243 Scroll Audit

Audit finding (verbatim, docs/feedback/items/243-scroll-audit/trades-surfaces.md):

"TradeValueBar is the single most expensive shared widget on every trade surface (label + heading + margin sentence + track + scale + end labels + a bordered verdict paragraph), and it appears twice in single-pin mode." — nominal height ≈240–248pt, cited against TradeValueBar.tsx:178-248.

Top-10 list items #4 and #6 both target this component: "collapse verdict paragraph behind a disclosure (~68pt × every instance, app-wide)" and "fontSize:9 → 11px floor fix (violation, trivial)". This page mocks both fixes plus a more aggressive third option, rendered in situ inside a faithful mini TradeCard wrapper (give/receive rows above the bar, no StrengthBar — matching how FeaturedTradeWindow.tsx mounts it in single-pin mode) so relative card height is directly visible.

Chalkline violation: TradeValueBar.tsx:223,227 hardcode fontSize: 9 on the scale labels — below the documented 11px floor. Fixed to 11px in every proposed variant below.

All pt math below uses the component's real token values (mobile/src/theme/chalkline.ts: space.xs=4 / sm=8 / md=12, type.label 11/14, type.heading 22/26, type.title 16/22, type.bodySm 13/18) and React Native's gap-plus-margin compounding (a parent gap and a child's own marginTop both apply and stack — not one-or-the-other). Assumes the verdict sentence wraps to 2 lines at 393pt width and the margin sentence stays on 1 line, both verified visually in the CURRENT frame below.

Recommendation

  • Ship V1 first. It's exactly what the #243 audit already itemized (winner-line step, verdict disclosure, tightened padding, the 11px floor fix), touches nothing about what information exists or in what order, and saves 56pt (23%) by default with zero information loss — the full verdict sentence is one tap away, same content as today.
  • V2 is the stronger structural lever (74pt / 30%) but it's a real content decision, not just a density pass: dropping the who-wins end row and merging margin+verdict changes what a user sees without tapping anything. Worth prototyping specifically for the single-pin featured card (#216/#209), where #243 notes the bar renders twice per screen — V2's extra ~18pt/instance saving over V1 compounds to ~36pt across both mounts.
  • The 11px scale-label fix costs nothing structurally (0pt height delta, confirmed in both pt tables above) — it should land regardless of which density variant ships, and could ship on its own today as the "trivial" item #6 the audit already flagged.
  • Not addressed here: the #243 top-10's #1 item (collapsing the single-pin Controls Card, ~240-250pt) and #2 (conditional deckWrap minHeight, ~240pt) are the two largest single-pin levers in the full audit — this page is scoped to the TradeValueBar component only, per the task.
