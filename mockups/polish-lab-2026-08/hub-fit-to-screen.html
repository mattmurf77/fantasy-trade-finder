What changed & why

  • LeaguePill switches to its existing compact variant (padding 12/16→8/12, name 16→13): −17pt with zero new code. (If the #223 header-league-switcher ships, this row disappears entirely and the page gains another ~50pt.)
  • Trade DNA: Chasing/Shopping labels move inline (label column beside the chips instead of stacked above), padding 12→10, inner gap 8→6: −34pt while both groups and every chip remain visible.
  • Mode cards: vertical padding 12→9, icon well 40→34 (icon 22→20): −8pt per card ×4. Title/body type untouched, so card hierarchy and tap targets (still ≥58pt tall) hold.
  • Nothing deleted, nothing reordered — total ≈ −120pt vs an observed overflow of ~40pt, so the fix also absorbs Dynamic-Type growth and 2-line league names without the scroll returning.
