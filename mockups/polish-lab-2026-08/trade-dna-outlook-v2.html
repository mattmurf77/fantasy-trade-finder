What changed from v1, and the interaction rules

  • Compact by default. Trade DNA is read a hundred times for every one edit. The collapsed card carries the full state in 111px — outlook chip with its plain-words bias, inline chasing/shopping position chips, untouchables count — a ~244px (−69%) saving vs the v1 panel, which puts all three finder modes back above the fold.
  • One affordance when collapsed. Edit is the only control (the whole card is also tappable to expand). No inline Manage links, no tappable chips in the summary — nothing to learn until you opt into editing.
  • Expand in place, not a sheet. Edit grows the same card (200ms, --t-base family); Done collapses it. Scroll position is preserved; the receipt's "Change" link deep-links here and auto-expands.
  • All positions, always visible (operator's ask). Chasing and Shopping each show QB · RB · WR · TE · Picks as one row of toggles. Selected = solid position-color fill + check glyph; unselected = outline (--line-strong) + position dot. One tap toggles; no add/remove flows, no picker sheet. Check + fill together keep state legible without relying on color alone.
  • Mutual exclusion. A position can't be both chased and shopped: tapping a position on one row that's selected on the other moves it (selects here, clears there) rather than erroring — the clearing button animates out its fill so the move is visible. Multi-select within a row is fine (chase WR + TE simultaneously).
  • Hints are not selections (#206 kept). The roster scan's STARTER NEED / DEPTH recommendations render as passive 11px tags in the reserved line under the relevant toggle — flare NEED for a lineup hole, dim DEPTH/DEEP for bench strength — with one key line defining both. Tags aren't tappable and persist whether or not the position is selected (compressed from v1's "Starter need" to fit the type floor under a 60px button; the key line carries the full definition).
  • Outlook cards survive, tighter. The v1 four-card grid was right for editing — it lives only in the expanded state now, at reduced padding/type (13px titles, 11px bias lines). Tap still switches directly.
  • Receipt untouched. The deck bias receipt frame is the v1 design verbatim — approved, no iteration.
  • Build-time note. Dark-on-fill text passes 4.5:1 on QB/RB/TE/Picks fills; WR blue (#3B82F6) sits at ≈4.1:1 — at implementation either bump the WR selected label to 14px bold (large-text threshold) or pair the fill with the check glyph as the primary state cue, per the a11y floor in the design system.
