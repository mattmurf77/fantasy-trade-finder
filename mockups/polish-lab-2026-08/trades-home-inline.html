#272's icons — what actually exists in code

Bottom tab-bar icons render via tabIcon() in mobile/src/navigation/TabNav.tsx:513 — <Icon size={22}>, flat, no per-tab override. Two of the operator's three named surfaces are real icon-bearing tabs today: Draft (glyph flag) and Trade (tab labeled "Acquire" since the #245 rename, glyph trade) — both 22pt. The third, Free Agents, has no icon anywhere in the app today: it's a text-only chip in TradeFinderModeBar (CHIPS array, components/TradeFinderModeBar.tsx:40-46) and a pushed screen with no tab-bar presence at all — mobile/src/components/chalkline/Icon.tsx's IconName union has no free-agents glyph. Proposed below: 22 → 28pt (+27%) for all three, and Free Agents borrows the existing search glyph (nearest semantic fit already in the shared set — "find available players") rather than inventing a new SVG, flagged honestly as a new-icon cost. All three now render together in an on-page utility row (below), not split across the tab bar and a scrolling chip strip.

#279's honest caveat, upfront

Team/positional totals (LeagueSummaryScreen.tsx's posValues/total_value) are sums across many assets — a real team total blows past the top tier's 4+ 1sts ceiling immediately (a competitive roster is worth many multiples of one top asset). The 8-tier ladder (TIER_LABEL/ORDERED_TIERS) is a per-asset classification with a fixed 8-bucket ceiling; it does not extend to aggregates without new calibration work. The dedicated frame at the end mocks the more honest option — reusing the existing value→pick-equivalent formula already live on every trade card ("a Late 2nd" in the Dynasty Value Swing bar, backend/server.py's _gap/pick_equivalent) scaled up to whole totals (e.g. "≈14 firsts") — rather than forcing a sum into one of the 8 discrete per-player buckets. See that frame's caption for what real binning work this would still need.

What's NOT solved here: the drill-in roster view (LeagueSummaryScreen.tsx:1027, per-player Math.round(p.value)) already has a clean answer — swap in the existing TierBadge, no new work, since those ARE single-asset values. The bar chart's stacked position segments have no numeric label today at all (color-proportion only) — extending pick-equivalent labels to sub-segments (e.g. "QB: ≈3 firsts" per team) wasn't attempted here; at small segment sizes a 4-way split of one "≈14 firsts" total starts to read as false precision, and nobody has picked a rounding/threshold rule for it. That's a real open design question, not just a build-cost one.
