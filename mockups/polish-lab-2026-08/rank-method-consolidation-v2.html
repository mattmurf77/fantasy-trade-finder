Rationale — v2 deltas

  • Most control = Tiers board (operator correction): "control" to a dynasty player means owning where every player SITS, and the tier board delivers that while staying survey-able — 8 buckets, not 250 ordered slots. The overall 1-to-N list is maximal effort, not maximal usefulness (the engine prices trades off tier-scale values, so slot-level order past the top of the board barely moves suggestions); it stays available as "Overall ranks" in the disclosure for the tinkerers.
  • Import copy — "Have rankings already? Import them": the operator's sentence ("I already have my rankings stored elsewhere") is the user's thought; compressing it to a dim question + ice verb keeps the thought and makes only the verb look tappable. Runner-up "Import my rankings" was rejected because bare, it reads like a fourth method ("import" as another way to build) — the question form self-selects: only people who HAVE rankings elsewhere engage. Rendered as one 36px bordered chip, a full visual tier below the cards.
  • Sheet before file picker: "Bring your rankings" shows the three methods AND the honesty line ("we'll match names…you review anything we can't match") before any OS picker opens — the review step is promised up front, so B3 lands as the deal the user accepted, not a failure state.
  • Match review is the real cost (B3): any import lives or dies on name matching against the league's Sleeper pool. Auto-accept close matches (suffixes, punctuation, "Kenneth Walker" → III) but show them for audit; ambiguous names get candidate chips + Skip; primary disabled until every row is matched or skipped; skipped/unlisted players keep their current board values. This screen exists in EVERY scope — it's the floor cost of the feature, not gold-plating.
  • Defer-if-complex scoping (operator's option): a v1 scope is paste-only — one textarea, one tolerant line parser, plus B3. It covers the CSV case for free (open file → copy → paste) with no file-picker, MIME or encoding work. CSV upload is a small follow-on (same parser behind a document picker); XLSX is the deferrable chunk — it needs a real parser dependency (SheetJS or server-side openpyxl) and multi-sheet/column-picking UX. If even paste feels heavy, the whole entry chip can ship later: nothing else on the chooser depends on it.
  • Carried from v1 unchanged: one chooser rendered on both surfaces (screen + Rank-tab sheet), outcome labels (Fastest / Most precise / Most control), Quick rank as Quick set's follow-on pass rather than a peer, and the #233 "Continue — no QBs this high" empty-tier CTA with Skip hidden at 0 selected.
