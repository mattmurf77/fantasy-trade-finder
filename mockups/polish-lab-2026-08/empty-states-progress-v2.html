Kept for reference only — v1's proposed order and the flat fairness meter the operator asked to replace. Full page: empty-states-progress.html.

Rationale — what changed from v1 and why

  • Explore first: the two Explore rows (League rankings · Free agents) are the destinations that already work with zero setup, so they now sit directly under the League card in both variants — day-one value is one tap from the top.
  • Progress before the example: League progress moves above "Works right now", making the unfinished-foundation story (ring + leaguemate bar + unlock sentence) the page's spine; the example trade becomes supporting evidence, not the lead.
  • Real value bar on the example (fidelity to TradeValueBar.tsx): diverging bar centered on Even spanning ±1 Mid 1st; fill = gap.firsts (0.6 → 30% of the track, rightward), colored --pos because favors=receive; inner ticks at the ±2nd landmark (SECOND_IN_FIRSTS = 0.57 → 21.5% / 78.5%); 9px mono scale labels (−1st · −2nd · Even · +2nd · +1st); They win / You win end labels with the winning side tinted; margin line "by +380 · a Mid 2nd (≈ 0.6 firsts)" with the pick name in the 1st-tier teal; counteroffer verdict strip with the 3px winner-colored left border. Replaces v1's flat "Fairness 88" meter. Still explicitly labeled EXAMPLE — not from your league (flare).
  • Terse CTAs, no descriptions: "Start Quick set" + two lines of copy becomes Rank players; the "Find trades today…" title/body above the example is gone — Find a trade stands alone. Both cards get meaningfully shorter.
  • A vs B tradeoff: A keeps each action next to the thing it acts on (ring → Rank players, example → Find a trade). B fronts both actions in one equal-width row under the League card — fastest possible access, at the cost of separating the buttons from their context; the in-section buttons are removed in B so no action appears twice, and only "Find a trade" keeps the ice fill (one primary per screen).
  • Unchanged from v1: the Matches empty-state proposal (section B of v1) is untouched by this feedback and is not repeated here; all zero-row collapsing rules (#229/#230/#234) carry over as specced in v1.
