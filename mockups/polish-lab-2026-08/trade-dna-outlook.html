One quiet receipt line above the deck (flare tick = informational, ADR-005): "Leaning young + picks — you're Rebuilding." The ice Change link jumps straight to the outlook cards. Same construction as the shipped "Built from your updated board" note, so it costs no new pattern.

Why this design

  • Edit is a button, not a whisper (#212). The panel's edit affordance becomes a bordered secondary button with a pencil glyph — the Chalkline secondary-button spec — and the outlook itself is directly tappable, so "you can change this" is self-evident with zero context.
  • Outlook reads as a choice, not a fact (#231). Four cards replace one line of body text; each names its bias in plain words ("Leans young + picks"), so picking one already teaches what it does — nothing to learn later.
  • The bias gets a receipt where it acts (#231). Results carry one sentence connecting deck shape to the setting, with an inline Change link — closing the loop between cause and effect on the same screen.
  • Two-tier needs without duplication (#206). STARTER NEED (flare) marks a lineup hole; DEPTH (dim) marks a thin bench; a chip carries at most one tag because starter need implies depth. One quiet key line defines both terms in league-speak.
  • Tenets check (#205): the panel still shows exactly three things (outlook, chasing, shopping); everything added is either a label made honest or a tap made obvious — no new data, no new concepts to explain.
