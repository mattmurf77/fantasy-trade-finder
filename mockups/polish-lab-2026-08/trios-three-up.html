Polish Lab · Trios Three-Up Reflow — #243 scroll audit, biggest rank-surface lever

Source: docs/feedback/items/243-scroll-audit/rank-surfaces.md — RankScreen (Trios / head-to-heads). The audit measured the screen's nominal state (no streak chip, no unlock-payoff copy, no Confirm button pre-selection, no unlocked banner) against the Rank-stack content budget: 852 − 59 (top inset) − 52 (global TopBar) − 44 (native stack header) − 49 (tab bar) − 34 (bottom inset) = 614pt.

"The three PlayerCards stacked vertically are 43% of total content height and by far the dominant line item… A horizontal 3-up row (each card ~1/3 width) would collapse this block from ~324pt to roughly one card's height (~110-130pt) — potential savings ~180-200pt, the single largest lever in this entire audit. Caveat: this needs a narrower card treatment (name truncation, badge wrapping at ~110pt width) — it's a real design pass, not a pure prop change, and readability at 1/3 width needs a mock before committing."

This file is that design pass. Row 1 recreates today's screen faithfully (mobile/src/screens/RankScreen.tsx + mobile/src/components/PlayerCard.tsx, classic branch — position rail, badge row, name, team·age·experience meta) at real token values, proving the audit's 747pt / 133pt-overflow math. Rows 2–4 mock three reflow treatments against the SAME trio (including one long name, Marvin Harrison Jr., tagged rookie — the audit's own "badge wrapping at ~110pt width" stress case) so truncation and tap-target behavior can be judged directly, not estimated.

Recommendation

V1 (3-up mini-cards) delivers the audit's full ~196pt lever with the most headroom (63pt spare — enough to absorb Dynamic Type growth or the streak chip/unlock banner the nominal-state audit deliberately excluded) and keeps the badge language (rank-1/2/3 medal fill, ice selected border) identical to today, so it's the lowest-risk path to the full savings. V3 is the same footprint with a livelier tapped-state — worth it only if product wants the extra affordance; it's an animation/polish layer on top of V1, not a competing structural choice, and both can ship from the same 115×128pt card. V2 is the safest visual delta (still literally "a list of three rows") but its 15pt margin is thin once any of the excluded chrome (streak chip ~52pt, unlock-payoff line, Confirm button) reappears — it would need its own further trim to hold the no-scroll line reliably. Open question for whichever ships: the name-truncation tradeoff is real at 115pt width — either accept it (full name stays available via the composed accessibility label and the existing long-press info sheet) or move the mini-card name to 2 lines instead of 1 (small change, adds ~14pt back to the card, still net a ~180pt win over today).
