Rationale — v3 deltas & recommendation

  • Recommendation: A (text link) — the tenets say quiet entry and one primary per screen, and a solid ice button to the right of the heading (B) genuinely competes with the three method cards' hierarchy: it's the only ice fill in view, so it out-shouts the recommended Quick set card the screen exists to sell. We agree with that concern, honestly stated on B's frame. A keeps the operator's self-selecting question ("Have rankings already?"), costs zero vertical space (it shares the heading's line), and the ice underline + glyph make it read as tappable without claiming primacy. If usage shows people with existing boards missing it, step up to C — outlined ice chip, still no fill — before ever going to B.
  • Fitted right, not stacked (operator's ask): the v2 entry was a full-width chip between heading and callout — a row of its own. All three v3 variants sit the entry in the heading's right margin: A baseline-aligned (text-to-text), B/C center-aligned (box-to-cap-height). Net −36px of chooser height vs v2.
  • Upload glyph corrected (operator's ask): arrow now points UP out of the tray (M8 9.5v-7 · M5 5 8 2l3 3 over the same tray path) — importing is the user handing rankings to us. The correction applies to every variant and, at build time, to the sheet rows' file icons if we ever add direction arrows there.
  • Wrap rule (shown on A): when heading + entry can't share 390px — long localized headings or large Dynamic Type — the row wraps and the intact entry right-aligns on its own line below. Never truncate the entry, never shrink the heading. C's one-word label is the most wrap-resistant; A wraps most often but degrades the most gracefully since it's plain text.
  • Unchanged from v2: the Bring-your-rankings sheet (CSV · XLSX · paste), honesty line, match-review screen with disabled-until-resolved primary, paste-only v1 scoping, Most control = Tiers board, and the #233 empty-tier CTA.
