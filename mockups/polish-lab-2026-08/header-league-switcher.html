What changed & why

  • League name replaces the wordmark in the global TopBar (11px "LEAGUE" micro-label over the name; brand survives as the ice tick). Users think in leagues, not app names — the header should answer "where am I trading?" on every tab.
  • Ice chevron-down beside the name = the click indicator (ice = action, per Chalkline). Whole left cluster is one ≥44pt hit target; pressed state = ink-3 fill, same as today's icon buttons.
  • Bell and gear stay put (bell moved inboard of gear so the unread badge sits away from the screen edge) — muscle memory for #topbar.settings / the bell is preserved.
  • One switcher, everywhere: the LeagueScreen hero keeps its card but drops the chevron affordance duty; the Trades LeaguePill row is removed, returning ~63pt to the hub (see hub-fit-to-screen.html). The sheet itself (LeagueSwitcherSheet incl. "Add a league") ships as-is.
  • Bar grows 44→52pt to fit the two-line label/name stack at the 11px type floor — cheaper than it looks, since the pill/hero rows it replaces cost 60+pt per screen.
