Same events, Chalkline construction: 20px stroke glyphs (match=ice, accept=pos, decline=neg, info=chalk-dim), titles lead with the fact, bodies carry only the new information (players · league). Unread rows take the specced flare 6px square + ink-2 fill.

What changed & why

  • Emoji removed at the source — every create_notification/_send_typed_push title in backend/server.py (🤝 ✅ ❌ 🎯 🎉 ⏳ 📰 👀 🏈 👋 🔥 🔓) drops its prefix, so the clients' defensive strip regexes become dead code instead of a band-aid. Design-system prohibition #1: no emoji in UI.
  • In-app rows adopt the specced NotificationRow (components.md): monochrome SVG glyph in the status color replaces the emoji's job; unread = flare 6px square + ink-2 row fill — signal moves from decoration to structure.
  • Copy tightened to the app's voice: title states the fact ("@murph accepted your trade"), body carries only new info (players · league). Kills the current title-repeated-in-body redundancy in match/disposition templates.
  • Push titles lose the hype register ("🔥 Trade matches are waiting" → "2 matches waiting") — the iOS banner already shows the app icon, so the title doesn't need a brand costume; counts move into the title where they scan fastest.
  • No layout or routing changes — sheet construction, tap-routing (notif.tap_routing_v2), Clear all, and timestamps are untouched; this is a copy + row-construction pass only.
