Where does draft handling live?

Operator ask: "We need to explore where to present the draft handling to users. Draft a few options — one such option would be to create a 5th tab at the bottom."

What is actually built and dark right now (verified on branch wave/integration, all flags OFF):

  • Rookie scope (ranks.rookie_subset) — one shared RookieScopeControl (All players | Rookies) on all six ranking modes, plus a consolidated cross-position RookieRanksScreen (route RookieRanks, deep link app/rank/rookies) entered from every scope control and a "Rookies" section on RankHome. Session-only, view-filter-only — same board, same Elo (D2).
  • Draft Room (draft.room + draft.live_poll) — a root-stack DraftRoomScreen: status bar (state + as_of + Refresh), designed notices (order-not-set / class-not-loaded / startup / platform-unsupported / stale / unavailable), Your picks, the board (slot · owner · pick), and Still-on-the-board with a Consensus | My-board toggle. Its only entry point today is the League-page Explore tile, which swaps from "Rookie board" to "Rookie draft" when draft.room is on.
  • Upcoming: M5 MFL parity and M6/M6b per-slot pick values (display first, then a #214-style engine toggle). Whatever we pick must not preclude either.

The crux is not "where does it fit" — it is "what does it look like when there is no draft." A rookie draft is live for roughly two to six weeks a year, in a window (late Apr → late Aug) that is the opposite of the window in which this app is otherwise most used. Every option below is judged first on its no-draft render, and only second on its live render. The frames come in pairs for exactly that reason.

Method notes. Frames are 393pt (iPhone 15/16-class). Fold math uses the #243 audit budget: 852 − 59 top inset − 52 TopBar − 49 tab bar − 34 bottom inset = 658pt usable. Chalkline tokens only (no emoji glyphs, no gradients, radius ≤ 8px, ice = actions, flare = informational). Position hexes are the cross-client data encoding, untouched.

A · Permanent 5th tab "Draft" Operator's named option
First, the fit question, answered honestly: it fits. At 393pt a 5-tab bar gives each tab 78.6pt (4 tabs = 98.25pt); on the narrowest supported device (iPhone SE, 375pt) it is 75pt. Both are far above the 44pt tap-target floor, and at the tab label size (Archivo 600 / 11px) the longest label in the set — "Acquire" and "Matches", 7 characters, ≈43pt — clears 78.6pt with ~35pt to spare. iOS HIG permits 5. So "it won't fit" is not a valid argument against this option, and I am not going to make it. The real costs are elsewhere: what the tab means when empty, and what it costs the four tabs it dilutes.

Recommendation
Ship B + D now. Keep C as the permanent home. Hold A′. Add E later. My call

The reasoning in one paragraph. A fifth tab is not too wide — at 393pt it renders cleanly at 78.6pt per tab and every label fits with room to spare, so the honest objection is not fit but meaning: a bottom tab is the app's standing claim about what it is for, and a rookie draft is true for two to six weeks in the one season when this app is otherwise quietest. The conditional version (A′) fixes that by never showing the empty render, but it pays for it in exactly the place this codebase is most fragile — TabNav's first-mount-only contracts (initialRouteName, #244's completion-aware launch routing, #245/#246's freshly re-landed Acquire semantics) plus a dual-registration of DraftRoom so deep links survive the tab's absence. That is permanent structural complexity bought for a seasonal payoff, and I would not spend it before we have a single real draft's worth of usage data. Meanwhile the two cheapest options are also the two most conceptually correct: B because the draft genuinely is the third acquisition channel and the Acquire chip strip already treats push-screens (Calc, Free agents) as peers of in-place modes, so a Draft chip costs one array entry and zero vertical fold; and D because the ranking half of draft prep is already shipped on the Rank tab, and the room's "My board" undrafted ordering literally reads the board those surfaces edit — placing the draft entry beside the rookie-scope control makes a causal relationship visible instead of leaving it as trivia. Together B and D cover the two moments that matter (during the draft: I'm acquiring; before it: I'm ranking), cost roughly one array entry plus two conditional rows, and leave C in place as the permanent, season-agnostic home for the recap — which is the one thing that legitimately should live somewhere forever. E is the right answer to the real live-draft problem (a pick landed and I'm on another screen), but it is a notifier, not a placement, and it needs draft.live_poll proven in the field plus a re-run of the #243 fold audit at 622pt before it earns a place in global chrome — so it ships second, not first. Revisit A′ after one real draft season: if the data shows users repeatedly hunting for the room mid-draft despite B, D, and E, the tab has earned itself; until then it is a large permanent bet on a small seasonal window.

Concretely, in order:

  • Now (flag flip + ~2 small diffs): keep the shipped League tile (C). Add the Draft chip to TradeFinderModeBar.CHIPS with an onDraft push (B). Add the second row to RankHomeScreen's rookieSection and the conditional link inside RookieScopeControl (D).
